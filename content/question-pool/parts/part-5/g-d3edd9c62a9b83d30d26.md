## 答案 / 题解

### 第 1 题

**答案：B**

During high school, Richard was ------- by his uncompromising determination
to win at any cost.
(A) distinguish 
(B) distinguished
(C) distinguishable (D) distinguisher
▶
在高中的时候，理查德就因其永不妥协、不惜一切获胜的决心而出
名。
▶
解析
 句意应为“理查德以…而与众不同”，故选 (B)
distinguished (与众不同的)。
