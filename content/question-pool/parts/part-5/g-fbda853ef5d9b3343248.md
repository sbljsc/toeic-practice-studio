## 答案 / 题解

##### 第 1 题

**答案：A**

The building management has introduced guidelines aimed at streamlining the
process of ------- existing leases.
(A) renewing
 (B) reacting
(C) reminding (D) returning
▶
大楼管理处推行了旨在简化现有出租续约流程的指导方针。
