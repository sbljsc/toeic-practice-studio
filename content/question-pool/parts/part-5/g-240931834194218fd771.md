### 第 1 题

The company chairman expressed disappointment in the behavior of some staff
members ------- said that most employees had followed company guidelines.

- A. and
- B. but
- C. or
- D. else

## 答案 / 题解

### 第 1 题

**答案：B**

The company chairman expressed disappointment in the behavior of some staff
members ------- said that most employees had followed company guidelines.
(A) and 
(B) but
(C) or (D) else
▶
公司董事长对有些员工的行为表示失望，但是他说大多数员工还是遵
守公司制度的。
▶
解析
 expressed disappointment in the behavior of some
staff members (对部分员工的行为表示失望) 和said that most
employees had followed company guidelines (说大多数员工遵守了公司
制度) 是转折关系，因此最恰当的是 (B) but。
