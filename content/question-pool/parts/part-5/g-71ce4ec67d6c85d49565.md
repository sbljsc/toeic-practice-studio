In the interests of consumer safety, the senior management unanimously -------

- D. established

## 答案 / 题解

##### 第 1 题

**答案：C**

In the interests of consumer safety, the senior management unanimously -------
to recall all versions of the Alphington and Executive sedans.
(A) settled (B) picked
(C) decided
 (D) established

为了消费者的安全起见，高层管理人员一致决定召回阿尔法顿和埃克

解析
