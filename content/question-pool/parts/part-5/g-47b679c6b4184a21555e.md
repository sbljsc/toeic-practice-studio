## 选项

##### 第 1 题

We can expect ------- temperatures and calm conditions in the central and
southern regions for at least the next three days.

- A. moderate
- B. moderates
- C. moderately
- D. moderation

## 答案 / 题解

##### 第 1 题

**答案：A**

We can expect ------- temperatures and calm conditions in the central and
southern regions for at least the next three days.
(A) moderate
 (B) moderates
(C) moderately (D) moderation
▶
我们可以预计，至少在接下来的三天中，中部和南部地区气温适中、
风和日丽。
▶
解析
 本题需选择词性恰当的词。横线处需填入修饰temperatures
(气温，温度) 的形容词。答案是 (A) moderate (温和的，适度的)。
