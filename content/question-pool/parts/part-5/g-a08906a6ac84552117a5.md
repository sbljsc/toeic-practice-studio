### 第 1 题

The Springborg Hotel chain’s attention to detail and business-friendly pricing
structure make our hotels ------- to any others in the mid-priced hotel bracket.

- A. better
- B. improved
- C. superior
- D. advanced

## 答案 / 题解

### 第 1 题

**答案：C**

The Springborg Hotel chain’s attention to detail and business-friendly pricing
structure make our hotels ------- to any others in the mid-priced hotel bracket.
(A) better (B) improved
(C) superior
 (D) advanced
▶
斯普林堡连锁酒店注重细节和利于经营的价格结构使我们的酒店在中
档价位酒店中处于优势地位。
▶
解析
 any others in the mid-priced hotel bracket(中档价位的
其他酒店)是比较对象，横线后有介词to，因此表示比较意义的形容词(C)
superior最恰当。
