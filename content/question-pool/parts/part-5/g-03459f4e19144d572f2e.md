## 选项

### 第 1 题

After you have exited the operating system and shut down the projector, please
flick the master power switch to the“-------” position.

- A. of
- B. to
- C. off
- D. over

## 答案 / 题解

### 第 1 题

**答案：C**

After you have exited the operating system and shut down the projector, please
flick the master power switch to the“-------” position.
(A) of (B) to
(C) off
 (D) over
▶
在您退出操作系统并关闭投影仪之后，请轻按电源总开关，使其处于
关闭状态。
▶
解析
 由状语从句after you have exited the operating system
and shut down the projector (在您退出操作系统并关闭投影仪之后) 和
master power switch (电源总开关) 这些内容可推断，使用完机器后应该
将其关闭，因此选择 (C) off。电脑或其他电器的电源开关一般标有“On
(开) / Off (关) ”。
