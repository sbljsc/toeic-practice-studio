## 选项

##### 第 1 题

Following media criticism ------- the Defense Secretary’s performance, the
President today spoke in his defense at a special press conference.

- A. at
- B. of
- C. for
- D. from

## 答案 / 题解

##### 第 1 题

**答案：B**

Following media criticism ------- the Defense Secretary’s performance, the
President today spoke in his defense at a special press conference.
(A) at 
(B) of
(C) for (D) from
▶
在媒体批评国防部长的表现之后，总统今天在特别新闻发布会上为他
做了辩护。
▶
解析
 the Defense Secretary’s performance (国防部长的表现)
是media criticism (媒体批评) 的对象，答案是介词 (B) of。
