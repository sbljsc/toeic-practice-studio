## 选项

##### 第 1 题

The social committee guarantees that this year’s company mystery adventure
tour will not fail to fascinate ------- surprise you.

- A. but
- B. and
- C. also
- D. even

## 答案 / 题解

##### 第 1 题

**答案：B**

The social committee guarantees that this year’s company mystery adventure
tour will not fail to fascinate ------- surprise you.
(A) but 
(B) and
(C) also (D) even
▶
社区委员会保证今年公司的神秘探险旅行一定会让人着迷和惊讶的。
▶
解析
 横线前后的fascinate和surprise都以句尾的you为宾语，且
语义为并列关系，因此横线处应填入连词(B)and。
