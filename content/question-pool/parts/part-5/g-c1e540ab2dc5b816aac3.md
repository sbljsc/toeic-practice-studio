## 选项

### 第 1 题

All employees are reminded that there is much satisfaction to be gained from -
------ the community in some voluntary capacity.

- A. serving
- B. deserving
- C. reserving
- D. preserving

## 答案 / 题解

### 第 1 题

**答案：A**

All employees are reminded that there is much satisfaction to be gained from --
----- the community in some voluntary capacity.
(A) serving
 (B) deserving
(C) reserving (D) preserving
▶
所有员工被提醒在某些志愿者工作中，为社区服务能获得很高的满意
度。
▶
解析
 横线处需要填入被in some voluntary capacity修饰、以the
community为宾语的动词，符合句意的是 (A) serving。
