## 选项

##### 第 1 题

While there has been a significant ------- in the sales figures, there has been no
corresponding profit growth because of increased production costs.

- A. boost
- B. addition
- C. forms
- D. promotion

## 答案 / 题解

##### 第 1 题

**答案：A**

While there has been a significant ------- in the sales figures, there has been no
corresponding profit growth because of increased production costs.
(A) boost
 (B) addition
(C) forms (D) promotion
▶
尽管销售额显著增长，但是因为生产成本的提高，利润没有相应地增
加。
▶
解析
 significant (显著的) 一般修饰具有“增加，减少，变
化”等词义的名词，句意应为“销售额的显著增长”，因此 (A) boost最恰
当。
