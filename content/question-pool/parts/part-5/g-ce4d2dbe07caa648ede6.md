## 选项

### 第 1 题

The In-Transit Travel Agency will do everything possible to ------- your stay
in Asia relaxing and enjoyable.

- A. do
- B. have
- C. give
- D. make

## 答案 / 题解

### 第 1 题

**答案：D**

The In-Transit Travel Agency will do everything possible to ------- your stay in
Asia relaxing and enjoyable.
(A) do (B) have
(C) give 
(D) make
▶
运通旅行社将竭尽所能使您的亚洲之旅轻松而愉悦。
▶
解析
 句意应为“使您的亚洲之旅轻松而愉悦”，答案是 (D)
make。make your stay in Asia relaxing and enjoyable是“make +宾语
(your stay in Asia) +宾语补足语 (relaxing and enjoyable) ”的结
构。
