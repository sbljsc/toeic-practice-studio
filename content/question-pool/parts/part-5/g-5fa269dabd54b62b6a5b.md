### 第 1 题

Government regulations state that no one can be ------- to the closed reading
room without first obtaining a security clearance.

- A. suited
- B. required
- C. admitted
- D. disposed

## 答案 / 题解

### 第 1 题

**答案：C**

Government regulations state that no one can be ------- to the closed reading
room without first obtaining a security clearance.
(A) suited (B) required
(C) admitted
 (D) disposed
▶
政府法规规定，没有进行安全审查之前不能进入这间不对外开放的阅
览室。
▶
解析
 根据no one can be，to the closed reading room，
without first obtaining a security clearance等内容，应选 (C)
admitted。
