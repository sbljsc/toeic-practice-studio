## 选项

##### 第 1 题

Despite being a newcomer to the field of shipbuilding, Upgrade Engineering
has achieved ------- results in its first year.

- A. envier
- B. envying
- C. enviable
- D. envyingly

## 答案 / 题解

##### 第 1 题

**答案：C**

Despite being a newcomer to the field of shipbuilding, Upgrade Engineering
has achieved ------- results in its first year.
(A) envier (B) envying
(C) enviable
 (D) envyingly
▶
尽管升级工程公司是造船业的新手，但它已经在创立的第一年取得了
让人羡慕的成绩。
▶
解析
 横线位于动词和宾语之间，需填入修饰宾语的形容词。答案
是 (C) enviable (令人羡慕的)。
