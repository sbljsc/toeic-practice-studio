## 选项

### 第 1 题

The giant pharmaceutical company insists that its new drug is ------- safe as
long as it used under the supervision of a doctor.

- A. perfect
- B. perfection
- C. perfectly
- D. perfecting

## 答案 / 题解

### 第 1 题

**答案：C**

The giant pharmaceutical company insists that its new drug is ------- safe as
long as it is used under the supervision of a doctor.
(A) perfect (B) perfection
(C) perfectly
 (D) perfecting
▶
这家制药巨头公司坚称其新药只要在医生的指导下服用就肯定是安全
的。
▶
解析
 省略横线处所填单词，本句也能构成完整结构，因此横线处
应填入副词。答案是 (C) perfectly。
