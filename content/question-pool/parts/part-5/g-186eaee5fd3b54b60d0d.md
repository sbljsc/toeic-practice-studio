## 选项

##### 第 1 题

Customers need to consider carefully ------- kind of interior fittings will best
satisfy both their needs and tastes.

- A. which
- B. since
- C. that
- D. as

## 答案 / 题解

##### 第 1 题

**答案：A**

Customers need to consider carefully ------- kind of interior fittings will best
satisfy both their needs and tastes.
(A) which
 (B) since
(C) that (D) as
▶
顾客需要仔细考虑哪种室内装修最满足他们的需求和品味。
▶
解析
 横线处所填单词修饰名词kind，且横线后应构成名词性从
句。答案是 (A) which。
