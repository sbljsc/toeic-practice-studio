After a highly profitable first six months, the restaurant’s profits for the second
half of the year were a great -------.

- A. disappoints
- B. disappointed
- C. disappointing
- D. disappointment

## 答案 / 题解

### 第 1 题

**答案：D**

After a highly profitable first six months, the restaurant’s profits for the second
half of the year were a great -------.
(A) disappoints (B) disappointed
(C) disappointing 
(D) disappointment
▶
在上半年创高收入后，这家餐厅下半年的收入非常令人失望。
▶
解析
 主系表结构中，名词和形容词可作表语，表语一般说明主语
的身份、特征、属性或状态。在本题中，主语是the restaurant’s
profits，横线前有冠词和形容词 (a great)，因此最恰当的是 (D)
disappointment。
