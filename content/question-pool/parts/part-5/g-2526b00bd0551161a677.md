## 选项

##### 第 1 题

The much-loved poet’s words could -------
be heard above the sound of thunderous applause.

- A. nearly
- B. barely
- C. sparsely
- D. singly

## 答案 / 题解

##### 第 1 题

**答案：B**

The much-loved poet’s words could ------- be heard above the sound of
thunderous applause.
(A) nearly 
(B) barely
(C) sparsely (D) singly
▶
那位备受爱戴的诗人的话在雷鸣般的掌声中几乎听不见。
▶
解析
 根据状语above the sound of thunderous applause (雷鸣
般的掌声中) 推断，诗人的讲话应该是被掌声淹没了。所以答案是具有否定
意义的 (B) barely (几乎不)。
