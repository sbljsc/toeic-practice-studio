## 选项

##### 第 1 题

------- Easter break, a skeleton staff of volunteers will maintain all essential
services to keep the business running.

- A. While
- B. Ahead
- C. During
- D. Since

## 答案 / 题解

##### 第 1 题

**答案：C**

------- Easter break, a skeleton staff of volunteers will maintain all essential
services to keep the business running.
(A) While (B) Ahead
(C) During
 (D) Since
▶
在复活节假期期间，志愿者中的骨干人员会维持所有的重要服务以保
证生意正常运营。
▶
解析
 Easter break (复活节假期) 是名词短语，因此横线处应填
入介词。在 (C) during (在…期间) 和 (D) since (自从) 间选择符合句
意的即可。答案是 (C) during。
