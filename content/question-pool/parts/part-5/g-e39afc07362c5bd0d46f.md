## 选项

##### 第 1 题

------- way you look at the problem, it is clear that no solution can be found
without the creativity and commitment of the staff themselves.

- A. Whichever
- B. Since
- C. Which
- D. That

## 答案 / 题解

##### 第 1 题

**答案：A**

------- way you look at the problem, it is clear that no solution can be found
without the creativity and commitment of the staff themselves.
(A) Whichever
 (B) Since
(C) Which (D) That
▶
无论你用哪种方式看待问题，很显然，要解决问题离不开全体员工的
创造力和投入。
▶
解析
 主句是it is clear that no solution...staff
themselves，因此______ way you look at the problem应构成状语从句。
排除引导名词性从句的 (C) which和 (D) that。(B) since (自…以来，…
以后) 是引导状语从句的从属连词，但是与主句语意不连贯。句意应为“无
论用哪种方式看待问题”，最恰当的是 (A) whichever (无论哪个)。
