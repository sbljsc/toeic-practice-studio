## 选项

### 第 1 题

The Department of Energy announced that the president ------- will make a
tour of the new facility when it opens a week from today.

- A. itself
- B. herself
- C. oneself
- D. myself

## 答案 / 题解

### 第 1 题

**答案：B**

The Department of Energy announced that the president ------- will make a tour
of the new facility when it opens a week from today.
(A) itself 
(B) herself
(C) oneself (D) myself
▶
能源部宣布主席会在一周后新厂开张时亲自前往考察。
▶
解析
 横线位于主语和动词之间，因此横线处需填入强调主语的反
身代词。答案是强调president的反身代词 (B) herself。
