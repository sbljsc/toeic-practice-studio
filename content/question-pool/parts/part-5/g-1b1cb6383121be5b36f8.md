## 选项

### 第 1 题

Our multi-skilled CEO has distinguished ------- yet again by winning the
coveted Brookings Award for innovation in business.

- A. he
- B. his
- C. him
- D. himself

## 答案 / 题解

### 第 1 题

**答案：D**

Our multi-skilled CEO has distinguished ------- yet again by winning the
coveted Brookings Award for innovation in business.
(A) he (B) his
(C) him 
(D) himself
▶
我们多才多艺的首席执行官赢得了人人向往的商界创新奖布鲁金斯
奖，再一次证明了他自身的杰出能力。
▶
解析
 横线处所填代词指代主语CEO，故选反身代词(D)himself。
distinguish oneself是“使自身杰出”的意思。
