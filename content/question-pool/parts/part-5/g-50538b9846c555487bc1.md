## 选项

### 第 1 题

Unfortunately, several patients at the hospital have been struck by a disease for
------- there is no known cure.

- A. while
- B. whose
- C. which
- D. that

## 答案 / 题解

### 第 1 题

**答案：C**

Unfortunately, several patients at the hospital have been struck by a disease for
------- there is no known cure.
(A) while (B) whose
(C) which
 (D) that
▶
不幸的是，医院里的几位病人患了一种目前无法治愈的病。
▶
解析
 for ______ there is no known cure修饰disease，因此横
线处应填入关系代词。答案是 (C) which。(D) that引导定语从句时前面不
能有介词。
