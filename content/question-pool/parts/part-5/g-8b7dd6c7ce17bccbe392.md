## 选项

##### 第 1 题

------- that it is undesirable to treat our production line workers like robots,
we have introduced a policy of frequent job rotations.

- A. Recognize
- B. Recognized
- C. Recognizing
- D. Having been recognized

## 答案 / 题解

##### 第 1 题

**答案：C**

------- that it is undesirable to treat our production line workers like robots, we
have introduced a policy of frequent job rotations.
(A) Recognize (B) Recognized
(C) Recognizing
 (D) Having been recognized
▶
我们意识到把生产线工人像机器人一样对待是不得人心的，于是引入
了频繁的工作轮换制度。
▶
解析
 that引导的从句是横线处所填动词的宾语，且recognize的动
作实施者就是逗号后主句的主语we。因此______ that it is
undesirable... robots应是分词短语作状语，故选 (C) recognizing。
