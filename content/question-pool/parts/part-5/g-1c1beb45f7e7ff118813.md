## 答案 / 题解

### 第 1 题

**答案：C**

Designers are experimenting with a new material ------- flexibility with
lightness.
(A) is combining (B) has combined
(C) that combines
 (D) combination of
▶
设计者们正在试验一种将灵活性和轻盈性结合起来的新材料。
▶
解析
 横线前已经构成完整的结构，这说明______ flexibility
with lightness修饰material。横线处应填入 (C) that combines，构成定
语从句。
