## 选项

### 第 1 题

The company is pleased to announce that we have invited several members of
our experienced ------- and casual staff to become permanent employees.

- A. minor
- B. lengthy
- C. temporary
- D. duplicate

## 答案 / 题解

### 第 1 题

**答案：C**

The company is pleased to announce that we have invited several members of
our experienced ------- and casual staff to become permanent employees.
(A) minor (B) lengthy
(C) temporary
 (D) duplicate
▶
公司很高兴地宣布我们已经邀请了几位有经验的临时工和非正式员工
成为正式员工。
▶
解析
 并列连词and一般用于列举相同词性的词汇，因此应选择和
casual(非正规的)意思相近的形容词。答案是(C) temporary(临时的)。
