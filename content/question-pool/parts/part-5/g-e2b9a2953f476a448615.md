## 选项

##### 第 1 题

One of the city’s landmark department stores has filed for bankruptcy;
however there is no indication that the historic building ------- will be demolished
or redeveloped.

- A. it
- B. its
- C. it’s
- D. itself

## 答案 / 题解

##### 第 1 题

**答案：D**

One of the city’s landmark department stores has filed for bankruptcy; however
there is no indication that the historic building ------- will be demolished or
redeveloped.
(A) it (B) its
(C) it’s 
(D) itself
▶
这个城市的一个地标性百货公司已经申请破产；尽管如此，没有迹象
表明这栋有历史意义的建筑将被拆除或重建。
▶
解析
 横线位于主语the historic building和谓语will be
demolished...之间，最恰当的是反身代词 (D) itself。强调主语实施的动
作或主语实施的动作影响到自身时，用反身代词。
