## 选项

##### 第 1 题

------- leaving school, she has worked in a variety of positions but has not yet
found one which suits her talents or interests.

- A. Despite
- B. Since
- C. In spite
- D. If

## 答案 / 题解

##### 第 1 题

**答案：B**

------- leaving school, she has worked in a variety of positions but has not yet
found one which suits her talents or interests.
(A) Despite 
(B) Since
(C) In spite (D) If
▶
自从毕业之后，她从事过很多种工作，但是至今仍未找到能发挥她才
能或者令她感兴趣的工作。
▶
解析
 横线前后没有leaving的主语，因此______ leaving school
是主语被省略的分词短语，故横线处应填入引导状语从句的从属连词。句意
应为“自从学校毕业后”，故选 (B) since。since leaving school=since
she left school。
