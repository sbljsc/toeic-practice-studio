## 选项

### 第 1 题

Creativity and productivity can be increased if our employees are able to
spend ------- time doing repetitive and routine tasks.

- A. least
- B. less
- C. lesser
- D. lessen

## 答案 / 题解

### 第 1 题

**答案：B**

Creativity and productivity can be increased if our employees are able to
spend ------- time doing repetitive and routine tasks.
(A) least 
(B) less
(C) lesser (D) lessen
▶
如果我们的员工能少做些重复性日常工作，那么他们的创造力和生产
效率就能提高。
▶
解析
 横线处应填入修饰time的形容词。先排除动词(D)lessen(缩
小，减轻)。(A)least是最高级，前面应该有定冠词the。(C)lesser是表
示“较少的，较小的，次要的”的形容词，也可以构成复合词，如lesser-
known(不大知名)。表示“量少”的little的比较级(B)less为答案。
