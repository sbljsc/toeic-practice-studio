## 选项

### 第 1 题

The prices charged by your Accounting Department do not match those -------
to me by your Sales Department at the time of purchase.

- A. repeated
- B. informed
- C. recorded
- D. quoted

## 答案 / 题解

### 第 1 题

**答案：D**

The prices charged by your Accounting Department do not match those -------
to me by your Sales Department at the time of purchase.
(A) repeated (B) informed
(C) recorded 
(D) quoted
▶
你们财务部收取的费用跟你们销售部在购买时给我的报价不符。
▶
解析
 those指代的是prices，“购买时销售部给我的报价”的句意
最恰当，故选 (D) quoted。quote有“引用，摘引”的词义，但在托业中一
般表示“报价，开价，出价”。
