## 选项

##### 第 1 题

The purpose of this planning meeting is to ensure that our move to the new site
will not cause any ------- in our services.

- A. illusion
- B. corruption
- C. instruction
- D. interruption

## 答案 / 题解

##### 第 1 题

**答案：D**

The purpose of this planning meeting is to ensure that our move to the new site
will not cause any ------- in our services.
(A) illusion (B) corruption
(C) instruction 
(D) interruption
▶
这次规划会议的目的就是确保公司搬迁新址不会引起我们服务的中
断。
▶
解析
 本题需选择符合句意的名词。因our move to the new site
(搬迁新址)，可能会发生“服务的中断”。答案是 (D) interruption (中
断；干扰) 。
