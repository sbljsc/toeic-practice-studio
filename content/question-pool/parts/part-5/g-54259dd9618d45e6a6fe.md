## 选项

##### 第 1 题

We would like to thank all of our sponsors for their generous donations, and
we hope to have your ------- support in the future.

- A. connected
- B. consecutive
- C. continued
- D. ceaseless

## 答案 / 题解

##### 第 1 题

**答案：C**

We would like to thank all of our sponsors for their generous donations, and we
hope to have your ------- support in the future.
(A) connected (B) consecutive
(C) continued
 (D) ceaseless
▶
我们要感谢所有赞助者的慷慨捐助，我们希望未来能继续得到你们的
支持。
▶
解析
 句意应为“希望未来能继续得到…”，故选 (C)
continued。(D) ceaseless (不停的，无休止的) 指不中断的连续状态，不
适合在这种语境下使用。
