## 选项

### 第 1 题

The chief financial officer believes that we should maintain the present
course, and ------- his deputy.

- A. as to
- B. whereas
- C. so does
- D. as long as

## 答案 / 题解

### 第 1 题

**答案：C**

The chief financial officer believes that we should maintain the present course,
and ------- his deputy.
(A) as to (B) whereas
(C) so does
 (D) as long as
▶
首席财务官认为我们应该保持目前的状况，并且他的代表也这样认
为。
▶
解析
 要表示后者的情况与前者相同时，可以用“so +助动词+主
语”的句型。答案是 (C) so does。此时，so=that we should maintain
the present course，does=believes。
