## 选项

##### 第 1 题

In response to our question on overall customer satisfaction, 23% of
respondents indicated that they were somewhat or very -------.

- A. dissatisfy
- B. dissatisfied
- C. dissatisfying
- D. are dissatisfied

## 答案 / 题解

##### 第 1 题

**答案：B**

In response to our question on overall customer satisfaction, 23% of
respondents indicated that they were somewhat or very -------.
(A) dissatisfy 
(B) dissatisfied
(C) dissatisfying (D) are dissatisfied
▶
在回答我们关于整体客户满意度的问题时，23%的受访者表明他们有
些或非常不满意。
▶
解析
 本题考查分词 (现在分词或过去分词) 作表语。分词作表语
时，如果主语与表语为被动关系，用过去分词；如果是主动关系，则用现在
分词。they (=23% of respondents) 和dissatisfy (使不满；使不服) 是
被动关系，故选 (B) dissatisfied。
