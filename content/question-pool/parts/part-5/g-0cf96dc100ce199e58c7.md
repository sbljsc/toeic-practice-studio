## 选项

##### 第 1 题

Anti tax-reduction lobbyists claim that the proposed tax cuts will ------- only
the richest at the expense of social welfare programs.

- A. attend
- B. dispose
- C. benefit
- D. compose

## 答案 / 题解

##### 第 1 题

**答案：C**

Anti tax-reduction lobbyists claim that the proposed tax cuts will ------- only
the richest at the expense of social welfare programs.
(A) attend (B) dispose
(C) benefit
 (D) compose
▶
反对减少税收的游说者声称，减税的提议只会在损坏社会福利制度的
情况下让富人从中获利。
▶
解析
 根据that从句的主语tax cuts (减税) 和宾语only the
richest (只有富人)，选择最符合句意的动词 (C) benefit (有益于)。
