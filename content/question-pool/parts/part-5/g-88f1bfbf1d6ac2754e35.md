## 选项

##### 第 1 题

Many specialists believe ------- the housing boom will continue for some time
unless the government intervenes.

- A. if
- B. that
- C. what
- D. which

## 答案 / 题解

##### 第 1 题

**答案：B**

Many specialists believe ------- the housing boom will continue for some time
unless the government intervenes.
(A) if 
(B) that
(C) what (D) which
▶
很多专家认为房产热将会持续一段时间，除非政府采取干预措施。
▶
解析
 the housing boom will continue for some time是believe
的宾语从句，因此横线处应填入引导宾语从句的从属连词 (B) that。
