## 选项

### 第 1 题

Our new range of products is available in ------- configurations, including
sports and commercial styles.

- A. vary
- B. variety
- C. variable
- D. various

## 答案 / 题解

### 第 1 题

**答案：D**

Our new range of products is available in ------- configurations, including
sports and commercial styles.
(A) vary (B) variety
(C) variable 
(D) various
▶
我们的新产品有各种规格，包括运动类和商业类。
▶
解析
 横线处需要填入修饰名词configurations的形容词。答案是
(D)various(各种各样的)。
