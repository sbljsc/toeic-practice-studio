## 选项

##### 第 1 题

All tenants may access the building outside of normal office hours by entering
------- the security door on the south side.

- A. on
- B. upon
- C. through
- D. between

## 答案 / 题解

##### 第 1 题

**答案：C**

All tenants may access the building outside of normal office hours by entering -
------ the security door on the south side.
(A) on (B) upon
(C) through
 (D) between
▶
在非办公时间，所有租户都可通过南侧的安全门进入大楼。
▶
解析
 enter表示“进入”，the security door (安全门) 是可通
行的关口，因此最恰当的是 (C) through (穿过，经过)。
