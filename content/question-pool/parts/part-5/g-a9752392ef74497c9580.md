## 选项

### 第 1 题

The high resolution camera introduced last December has ------- been
superseded as a result of recent technological developments.

- A. so
- B. already
- C. such
- D. yet

## 答案 / 题解

### 第 1 题

**答案：B**

The high resolution camera introduced last December has ------- been
superseded as a result of recent technological developments.
(A) so 
(B) already
(C) such (D) yet
▶
由于最近技术的发展，去年12月引进的高分辨率相机已经被淘汰了。
▶
解析
 本题需选择符合句意的副词。根据as a result of recent
technological developments和has ______ been superseded，最恰当的是
(B) already (已经，早已)。
