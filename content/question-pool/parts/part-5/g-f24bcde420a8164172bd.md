## 选项

##### 第 1 题

It seems certain ------- our industry will be faced with multiple challenges in
the near and medium term, principally because of changes in the international
political landscape.

- A. because
- B. that
- C. for
- D. so

## 答案 / 题解

##### 第 1 题

**答案：B**

It seems certain ------- our industry will be faced with multiple challenges in
the near and medium term, principally because of changes in the international
political landscape.
(A) because 
(B) that
(C) for (D) so
▶
看起来我们的工业在中短期内将面临多重挑战，这其中最主要的原因
在于国际政治局势的转变。
▶
解析
 横线后已构成完整的结构，句首的it指代的并不是特定的名
词或名词短语，而是后面的句子，作形式主语，句子结构应为It seems
certain + that 从句。引导名词性从句的连词 (B) that正确。
