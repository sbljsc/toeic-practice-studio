## 选项

### 第 1 题

It won’t be ------- to take notes during the seminar, as we will be handing out
detailed summaries.

- A. basic
- B. compelling
- C. necessary
- D. natural

## 答案 / 题解

### 第 1 题

**答案：C**

It won’t be ------- to take notes during the seminar, as we will be handing out
detailed summaries.
(A) basic (B) compelling
(C) necessary
 (D) natural
▶
在研讨会期间没有必要记笔记，因为我们会分发详细摘要。
▶
解析
 根据原因状语从句as we will be handing out detailed
summaries (我们会分发详细摘要) 判断，句意应为“会议期间不需要记笔
记”。答案是 (C) necessary (必需的，必要的)。
