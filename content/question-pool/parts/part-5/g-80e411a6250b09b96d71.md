## 选项

##### 第 1 题

The new summer collection ------- the Asiatic designs will be modeled in the
Women’s Fashion Department on Friday morning.

- A. about
- B. within
- C. over
- D. from

## 答案 / 题解

##### 第 1 题

**答案：D**

The new summer collection ------- the Asiatic designs will be modeled in the
Women’s Fashion Department on Friday morning.
(A) about (B) within
(C) over 
(D) from
▶
来自亚洲设计的新夏装将于周五早上在女性时尚部进行走秀展示。
▶
解析
 collection在这里表示新服装的发布会，或发布会上所展示
的服装，the Asiatic designs是collection的出处，因此 (D) from最恰
当。
