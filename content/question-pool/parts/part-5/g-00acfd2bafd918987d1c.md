## 选项

### 第 1 题

In today’s rapidly changing world, no professional can ------- to pass up the
opportunity to upgrade his or her qualifications.

- A. help
- B. leave
- C. afford
- D. include

## 答案 / 题解

### 第 1 题

**答案：C**

In today’s rapidly changing world, no professional can ------- to pass up the
opportunity to upgrade his or her qualifications.
(A) help (B) leave
(C) afford
 (D) include
▶
在如今瞬息万变的世界里，没有哪个专业人士能负担得起错过提升自
己能力的机会。
▶
解析
 横线处所填单词以不定式to pass up为宾语，因此排除 (B)
leave和 (D) include。最符合句意的是 (C) afford。can afford to do表
示“负担得起做某事”。
