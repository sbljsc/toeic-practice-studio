## 选项

### 第 1 题

Even professional marketers are never sure ------- some new products are
runaway successes while others fail miserably.

- A. for
- B. why
- C. since
- D. whoever

## 答案 / 题解

### 第 1 题

**答案：B**

Even professional marketers are never sure ------- some new products are
runaway successes while others fail miserably.
(A) for 
(B) why
(C) since (D) whoever
▶
甚至连专业市场分析员也不能确定为什么一些新产品能迅速获得成功
而另一些却惨遭失败。
▶
解析
 sure的用法为be sure of / about sth. 或be sure +从句。
答案是(B)why。
