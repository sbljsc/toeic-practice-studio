## 选项

### 第 1 题

We’d like to congratulate our gifted head of personnel, who won a gold medal
at the state-wide archery competition ------- over the weekend.

- A. held
- B. holds
- C. holding
- D. was held

## 答案 / 题解

### 第 1 题

**答案：A**

We’d like to congratulate our gifted head of personnel, who won a gold medal
at the state-wide archery competition ------- over the weekend.
(A) held
 (B) holds
(C) holding (D) was held
▶
我们祝贺有才华的人事部主任在周末举行的全国箭术比赛中获得金
牌。
▶
解析
 横线处所填单词修饰competition。能够修饰名词的动词词形
只能是现在分词或过去分词。名词和分词之间如果是被动关系，用过去分
词；如果是主动关系，用现在分词。competition(比赛)和hold(举行)之间
是被动关系，因此选择过去分词(A)held。
