## 选项

##### 第 1 题

Should the alarm sound, please ------- make your way to the rallying points,
where safety wardens will direct you to the emergency exits.

- A. calmly
- B. usually
- C. inherently
- D. increasingly

## 答案 / 题解

##### 第 1 题

**答案：A**

Should the alarm sound, please ------- make your way to the rallying points,
where safety wardens will direct you to the emergency exits.
(A) calmly
 (B) usually
(C) inherently (D) increasingly
▶
警报响起的时候，请镇定地前往聚集点，安全督导员将指引你们到紧
急出口。
▶
解析
 选择符合句意的副词。根据条件状语从句should the alarm
sound，可判断(A)calmly(镇定地，冷静地)最恰当。向对方郑重地命令或指
示时，一般用“please + 动词原形”。
