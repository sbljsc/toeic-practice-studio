### 第 1 题

All drivers are required to maintain ------- logbooks accurately and to hand
them in to the central office before leaving on Friday night.

- A. its
- B. his
- C. ours
- D. their

## 答案 / 题解

### 第 1 题

**答案：D**

All drivers are required to maintain ------- logbooks accurately and to hand
them in to the central office before leaving on Friday night.
(A) its (B) his
(C) ours 
(D) their
▶
所有的司机都要正确地记录他们的行驶日志并且在周五晚上离开前上
交给主办公室。
▶
解析
 logbooks表示“行驶日志”，因此横线处应填入指代drivers
的形容词性物主代词 (D) their。
