## 选项

### 第 1 题

Please place your luggage in the overhead locker, and ensure that the locker is
closed -------.

- A. secure
- B. securely
- C. securable
- D. secureness

## 答案 / 题解

### 第 1 题

**答案：B**

Please place your luggage in the overhead locker, and ensure that the locker is
closed -------.
(A) secure 
(B) securely
(C) securable (D) secureness
▶
请把行李放在上方的柜子里，并确保柜子已锁好。
▶
解析
 横线前已经构成完整的结构，因此横线处应填入修饰closed
的副词。答案是 (B) securely。
