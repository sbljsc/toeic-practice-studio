### 第 1 题

Talentspotters Agency is now ------- fashion models and actors with portfolios
who wish to work in advertising, film, or television.

- A. looking
- B. seeking
- C. extending
- D. equipping

## 答案 / 题解

### 第 1 题

**答案：B**

Talentspotters Agency is now ------- fashion models and actors with portfolios
who wish to work in advertising, film, or television.
(A) looking 
(B) seeking
(C) extending (D) equipping
▶
星探经纪人公司目前正在寻找那些愿意拍广告、演电影或者电视节目
的有应聘写真集的时装模特和演员。
▶
解析
 本题需选择适当的动词。主语是Talent-spotters Agency
(星探经纪人公司)，宾语是fashion models and actors (时装模特和演
员)。最符合句意的是 (B) seeking。
