internal design conference, the president spoke in general ------- about

- C. names
- D. terms

## 答案 / 题解

##### 第 1 题

**答案：D**

In his opening remarks at the company’s internal design conference, the
president spoke in general ------- about developments in the business environment.
(A) marks (B) expressions
(C) names 
(D) terms
▶
在公司内部设计会议的开场白中，总裁大概描述了一下公司在商业环
境中的发展状况。
▶
解析
 in general ______是修饰spoke的状语，答案是 (D)
terms。in general terms意为“概括地，笼统地”。
