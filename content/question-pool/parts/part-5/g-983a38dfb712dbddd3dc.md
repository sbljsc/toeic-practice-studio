## 答案 / 题解

### 第 1 题

**答案：B**

Marketing specialists are really not sure ------- changes in the climate will
affect sales of recreational vehicles.
(A) where 
(B) how
(C) what (D) who
▶
市场专家真的不能确定气候变化会怎样影响野营车的销售。
▶
解析
 sure 的用法有be sure of / about sth.或be sure +从句。
答案是(B)how。本题与第106题题型相同。
