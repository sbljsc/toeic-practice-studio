## 选项

### 第 1 题

The CEO of Griffin Investments said today that he had absolutely no hesitation
in ------- that his company had always operated within strict, ethical guidelines.

- A. affirming
- B. completing
- C. orienting
- D. expanding

## 答案 / 题解

### 第 1 题

**答案：A**

The CEO of Griffin Investments said today that he had absolutely no hesitation
in ------- that his company had always operated within strict, ethical guidelines.
(A) affirming
 (B) completing
(C) orienting (D) expanding
▶
格里芬投资公司的首席执行官今天毫不犹豫地肯定公司一直都在执行
严格的道德指导方针。
▶
解析
 本题需选择符合句意的动词。以that his company had
always operated within strict, ethical guidelines (他的公司一直都
在执行严格的道德指导方针) 为宾语，与he had absolutely no
hesitation in (他毫不犹豫地…) 语意连贯的是 (A) affirming (肯定)
。
