## 选项

### 第 1 题

A spokesman for the board of directors announced that the chairman will step
down ------- and when it becomes necessary.

- A. if
- B. there
- C. then
- D. so

## 答案 / 题解

### 第 1 题

**答案：A**

A spokesman for the board of directors announced that the chairman will step
down ------- and when it becomes necessary.
(A) if
 (B) there
(C) then (D) so
▶
董事会的一位发言人宣布如果有必要的话，董事长会让位。
▶
解析
 并列连词and连接并列、对等的语法成分。因此横线处应填入
与when对等的从属连词。答案是 (A) if。
