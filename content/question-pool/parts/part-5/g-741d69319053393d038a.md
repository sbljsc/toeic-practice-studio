## 选项

### 第 1 题

Senior managers assured investigators that ------- no time were they aware of
illegal activities in their Accounting Department.

- A. at
- B. over
- C. by
- D. in

## 答案 / 题解

### 第 1 题

**答案：A**

Senior managers assured investigators that ------- no time were they aware of
illegal activities in their Accounting Department.
(A) at
 (B) over
(C) by (D) in
▶
高层经理向调查员保证他们对财务部的不法行为毫不知情。
▶
解析
 在本文中，that从句的语序是“谓语+主语”，而非“主语
+谓语”，可见采用了倒装，因此______ no time应作状语。句意应为“他
们对财务部的不法行为毫不知情”，故选 (A) at。at no time意为“绝
不，从不”。
