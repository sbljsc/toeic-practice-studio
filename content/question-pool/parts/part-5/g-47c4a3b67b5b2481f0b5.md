## 选项

##### 第 1 题

The investigating panel is ------- out of patience with senior company
executives, who are blocking the release of essential documents.

- A. moving
- B. going
- C. breaking
- D. running

## 答案 / 题解

##### 第 1 题

**答案：D**

The investigating panel is ------- out of patience with senior company
executives, who are blocking the release of essential documents.
(A) moving (B) going
(C) breaking 
(D) running
▶
调查小组对正在阻止重要文件外泄的公司高级主管们快要失去耐心
了。
▶
解析
 主句句意应为“调查小组快要失去耐心了”，故选 (D)
running。run out of 表示“用完，耗尽”，类似的表达有run short
(of)。
