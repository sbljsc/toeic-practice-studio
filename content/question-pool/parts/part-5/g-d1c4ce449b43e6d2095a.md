## 答案 / 题解

### 第 1 题

**答案：B**

At the last meeting, many enquiries were made ------- the proposed shift in the
company’s mission statement.
(A) regard 
(B) regarding
(C) in regard (D) with regard

statement是enquiries (询问) 的对象，故选介词 (B) regarding (关
