## 选项

##### 第 1 题

Payment of telephone or Internet orders is ------- within 7 working days of the
receipt of the goods and invoice.

- A. due
- B. coming
- C. payable
- D. complete

## 答案 / 题解

##### 第 1 题

**答案：A**

Payment of telephone or Internet orders is ------- within 7 working days of the
receipt of the goods and invoice.
(A) due
 (B) coming
(C) payable (D) complete
▶
电话订购或者网上订购需要在收到货物和发票后的7个工作日内付
款。
▶
解析
 句意应为“应在收到货物和发票后7个工作日内付款”，故选
(A) due (应支付的)。
