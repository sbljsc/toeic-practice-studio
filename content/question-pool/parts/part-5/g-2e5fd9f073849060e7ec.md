## 选项

##### 第 1 题

The Department of the Environment supports the replacement of introduced
plants with ------- plants that require much less watering.

- A. native
- B. nativity
- C. natively
- D. nativeness

## 答案 / 题解

##### 第 1 题

**答案：A**

The Department of the Environment supports the replacement of introduced
plants with ------- plants that require much less watering.
(A) native
 (B) nativity
(C) natively (D) nativeness
▶
环境部门支持用需水量更少的本土植物来代替外来植物的做法。
▶
解析
 that require much less watering是修饰名词plants的定语
从句，横线前有with，因此横线处应填入形容词。答案是 (A) native。
