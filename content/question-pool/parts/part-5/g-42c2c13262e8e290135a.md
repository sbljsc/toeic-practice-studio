## 选项

### 第 1 题

It is strongly advised that you check that you have all your essential documents
in a briefcase ------- you set out on an international trip.

- A. upon
- B. before
- C. beside
- D. next to

## 答案 / 题解

### 第 1 题

**答案：B**

It is strongly advised that you check that you have all your essential documents
in a briefcase ------- you set out on an international trip.
(A) upon 
(B) before
(C) beside (D) next to
▶
我们强烈建议你在开始国际旅行前检查一下你所有的重要文件是否都
在公文包里。
▶
解析
 横线前后都是完整的结构，因此横线处需填入连词。答案是
(B) before，其余选项均为介词。
