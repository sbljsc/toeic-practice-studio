## 选项

##### 第 1 题

Our new director is not only highly qualified and experienced, ------- also has
a great deal of personal charm.

- A. he
- B. his
- C. him
- D. himself

## 答案 / 题解

##### 第 1 题

**答案：A**

Our new director is not only highly qualified and experienced, ------- also has
a great deal of personal charm.
(A) he
 (B) his
(C) him (D) himself
▶
我们的新经理不仅素质高、经验丰富，而且还有很大的个人魅力。
▶
解析
 横线处所填单词充当has的主语，故选人称代词的主格 (A)
he。
