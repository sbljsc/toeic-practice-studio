## 选项

### 第 1 题

Starlite Roofing Installation will accept responsibility for any damages -------
it can be proven that defective products were the cause of the accident.

- A. unless
- B. because of
- C. although
- D. insofar as

## 答案 / 题解

### 第 1 题

**答案：D**

Starlite Roofing Installation will accept responsibility for any damages -------
it can be proven that defective products were the cause of the accident.
(A) unless (B) because of
(C) although 
(D) insofar as
▶
如果能证明不合格产品是导致这起事故的原因，那么星光屋顶安装公
司将对所有损失负责。
▶
解析
 横线后是从句，故排除 (B) because of。句意应为“如果能
证实不合格产品是导致这起事故的原因”，因此答案是 (D) insofar as。
