## 选项

##### 第 1 题

The advertisements printed in this magazine do not ------- imply endorsement
by the management.

- A. highly
- B. barely
- C. gradually
- D. necessarily

## 答案 / 题解

##### 第 1 题

**答案：D**

The advertisements printed in this magazine do not ------- imply endorsement
by the management.
(A) highly (B) barely
(C) gradually 
(D) necessarily
▶
印在这本杂志上的广告不一定意味着管理者的认可。
▶
解析
 本题需选择符合句意的副词。横线处所填副词修饰动词
imply(包含，意味，暗示)，根据句意，选择(D)necessarily(必定，必然
地)。
