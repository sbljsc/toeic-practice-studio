## 选项

### 第 1 题

It is true that the number of traffic accidents involving cyclists has increased --
----- over the last 10 years.

- A. meagerly
- B. fundamentally
- C. significantly
- D. adequately

## 答案 / 题解

### 第 1 题

**答案：C**

It is true that the number of traffic accidents involving cyclists has increased ---
---- over the last 10 years.
(A) meagerly (B) fundamentally
(C) significantly
 (D) adequately
▶
确实，涉及骑自行车的人的交通事故在过去的十年内显著增长。
▶
解析
 横线处所填副词修饰has increased (增长了)，因此应表示
增加的程度，而且主语是the number of traffic accidents (交通事故的
数量)，最符合句意的副词是 (C) significantly (显著地)。
