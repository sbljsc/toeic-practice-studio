## 选项

##### 第 1 题

It is becoming apparent that all medium to large businesses will seek to -------
their operations by reducing the number of middle managers.

- A. streamline
- B. streamlines
- C. streamlined
- D. streamlining

## 答案 / 题解

##### 第 1 题

**答案：A**

It is becoming apparent that all medium to large businesses will seek to -------
their operations by reducing the number of middle managers.
(A) streamline
 (B) streamlines
(C) streamlined (D) streamlining
▶
越来越明显的是，所有大中型企业将试图通过减少中层管理者的数量
来提高运营效率。
▶
解析
 seek 可以接不定式作宾语，因此横线处应填入动词原形 (A)
streamline (使有效率；使增产节约)。
