## 选项

### 第 1 题

It has been ------- 40 years since our current president first walked through the
doors of this illustrious institution.

- A. entirely
- B. nearly
- C. ever
- D. most

## 答案 / 题解

### 第 1 题

**答案：B**

It has been ------- 40 years since our current president first walked through the
doors of this illustrious institution.
(A) entirely 
(B) nearly
(C) ever (D) most
▶
我们现任首脑第一次迈入这家著名机构的大门距现在已有近40年了。
▶
解析
 本题需选择符合句意的副词。句意应为“进入这家机构差不
多有40年”，故选 (B) nearly (接近，差不多)。entirely表示“完整地，
全部地”。
