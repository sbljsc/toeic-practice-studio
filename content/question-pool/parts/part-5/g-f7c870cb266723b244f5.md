## 选项

##### 第 1 题

Allways Engineering has guaranteed that ------- one of its employees will lose
his or her job as a result of the merger.

- A. no
- B. not
- C. none
- D. any

## 答案 / 题解

##### 第 1 题

**答案：B**

Allways Engineering has guaranteed that ------- one of its employees will lose
his or her job as a result of the merger.
(A) no 
(B) not
(C) none (D) any
▶
百达工程公司保证其任何一位员工都不会因为兼并而失业。
▶
解析
 (A)no表示not one，与横线后的one重复。(C)none表示not a
single one (of)，也与one重复。(D)any可与one 连用，但一般用于否定句
和疑问句，且不符合句意。(B)not是副词，一般用于not one of...的常用
结构。答案是(B)。no，not，none的用法容易混淆，要学会正确区分。
