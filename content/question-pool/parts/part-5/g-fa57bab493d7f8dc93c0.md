The Technical Department is currently formulating written guidelines -------

- A. in
- B. for

## 答案 / 题解

##### 第 1 题

**答案：B**

The Technical Department is currently formulating written guidelines ------- the
use of our micro-publishing facilities.
(A) in 
(B) for
(C) at (D) with
▶
技术部门目前正在为我们的微出版设施制订书面指南。
▶
解析
 本题需选择适当的介词。句意应为“为微出版设施制订使用
指南”，故选表示目的的介词 (B) for。
