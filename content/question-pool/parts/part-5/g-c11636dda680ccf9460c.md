## 选项

### 第 1 题

The unemployment rate ------- sharply despite government policies designed
to increase public sector employment.

- A. was rising
- B. will have risen
- C. has risen
- D. risen

## 答案 / 题解

### 第 1 题

**答案：C**

The unemployment rate ------- sharply despite government policies designed to
increase public sector employment.
(A) was rising (B) will have risen
(C) has risen
 (D) risen
▶
尽管政府出台政策增加公共部门的就业机会，但是失业率还是急剧上
升。
▶
解析
 句意应为“尽管政府…，失业率还是急剧上升”，主语the
unemployment rate与rise之间是主动关系，故选 (C) has risen。
