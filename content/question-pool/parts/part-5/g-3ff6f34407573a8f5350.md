## 选项

### 第 1 题

Media organizations can be called before a tribunal if they fail to operate -----
-- the guidelines laid out by federal legislation.

- A. within
- B. among
- C. between
- D. from

## 答案 / 题解

### 第 1 题

**答案：A**

Media organizations can be called before a tribunal if they fail to operate ------
- the guidelines laid out by federal legislation.
(A) within
 (B) among
(C) between (D) from
▶
如果媒体机构没有依循联邦立法方针运作，可能会被法院传唤。
▶
解析
 根据guidelines laid out by federal legislation(联邦立
法的方针)，可推测句意应为“媒体机构的运作不遵守联邦立法的方针时，
可能会被法庭传唤”，因此(A) within(在…之内)最恰当。
