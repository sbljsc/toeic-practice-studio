## 选项

##### 第 1 题

There can be no doubt that our Marketing Department has grown -------
confidence and ability with each new success.

- A. in
- B. with
- C. among
- D. between

## 答案 / 题解

##### 第 1 题

**答案：A**

There can be no doubt that our Marketing Department has grown -------
confidence and ability with each new success.
(A) in
 (B) with
(C) among (D) between
▶
毫无疑问，随着每一次新成功，我们市场部的信心和能力都得到了提
高。
▶
解析
 提高的具体范围是confidence and ability，故选表示特定
范围的介词 (A) in。
