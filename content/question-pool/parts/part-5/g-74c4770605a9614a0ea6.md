## 选项

### 第 1 题

The selection committee will consider applications from any employee -------
to apply for these positions.

- A. valuable
- B. impressive
- C. eligible
- D. flexible

## 答案 / 题解

### 第 1 题

**答案：C**

The selection committee will consider applications from any employee -------
to apply for these positions.
(A) valuable (B) impressive
(C) eligible
 (D) flexible
▶
选拔委员会将考虑来自任何有资格应聘这些职位的员工的申请。
▶
解析
 ______ to apply for these positions修饰any employee，
因此要表达出“有资格应聘这些职位”的句意。答案是 (C) eligible (合
格的，符合条件的)。
