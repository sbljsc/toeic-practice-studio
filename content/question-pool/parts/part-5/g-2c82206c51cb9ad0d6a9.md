## 选项

##### 第 1 题

Much of investors’ long-held esteem in the company ------- during the share-
market boom when executives were involved in accounting irregularities.

- A. losing
- B. loses
- C. was lost
- D. is lost

## 答案 / 题解

##### 第 1 题

**答案：C**

Much of investors’ long-held esteem in the company ------- during the share-
market boom when executives were involved in accounting irregularities.
(A) losing (B) loses
(C) was lost
 (D) is lost
▶
当领导层卷入做假账事件后，大多数投资者失去了在股票市场繁荣时
期建立起来的对公司的尊敬。
▶
解析
 横线处需填入句子的谓语。lose是及物动词，但横线后并没
有宾语，因此应为被动语态。状语从句when executives were involved in
accounting irregularities是过去时，按照句意主句也应是过去时，故选
(C) was lost。
