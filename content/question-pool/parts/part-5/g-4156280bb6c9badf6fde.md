## 选项

### 第 1 题

If we are to ------- towards our common goals, workers must have faith in
management, and vice versa.

- A. break
- B. advance
- C. growth
- D. approach

## 答案 / 题解

### 第 1 题

**答案：B**

If we are to ------- towards our common goals, workers must have faith in
management, and vice versa.
(A) break 
(B) advance
(C) growth (D) approach
▶
如果我们要实现我们的共同目标，工人们必须对管理部门有信心，反
之亦然。
▶
解析
 横线处应填入与介词towards (向，往，朝) 语意连贯的不及
物动词。答案是 (B) advance (前进，发展)。(D) approach (接近，靠近)
是及物动词，不与介词连用。
