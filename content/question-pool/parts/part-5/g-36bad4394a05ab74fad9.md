## 选项

### 第 1 题

I extend my sincere ------- to the family and friends of John Miller, who has
suddenly left us at the age of 54.

- A. appreciation
- B. description
- C. condolences
- D. charges

## 答案 / 题解

### 第 1 题

**答案：C**

I extend my sincere ------- to the family and friends of John Miller, who has
suddenly left us at the age of 54.
(A) appreciation (B) description
(C) condolences
 (D) charges
▶
54岁的约翰·米勒突然辞世，我对他的家人和朋友致以诚挚的哀悼。
▶
解析
 横线处填入的词被形容词sincere(真诚的)修饰，用来表示向
54岁突然辞世的约翰·米勒的朋友和家人致以哀悼，故(C)condolences(哀
悼)最恰当。
