## 选项

##### 第 1 题

If you are not sure which exercise schedule best suits your personal needs,
please ------- our detailed guidebook, or speak to one of our specialists.

- A. refer
- B. consult
- C. discuss
- D. consider

## 答案 / 题解

##### 第 1 题

**答案：B**

If you are not sure which exercise schedule best suits your personal needs,
please ------- our detailed guidebook, or speak to one of our specialists.
(A) refer 
(B) consult
(C) discuss (D) consider
▶
如果您不确定哪项健身计划最适合您的个人需要，请参考我们的详细
指南，或者咨询我们的专家。
▶
解析
 宾语是our detailed guidebook，因此横线处应填入具
有“参照，参考”词义的动词。答案是 (B) consult。(A) refer表示“参
考”时需与介词to连用。
