## 选项

##### 第 1 题

The organizing committee has not yet set a ------- date for the seminar, but it
will be some time in the first week of November.

- A. full
- B. factual
- C. explicit
- D. specific

## 答案 / 题解

##### 第 1 题

**答案：D**

The organizing committee has not yet set a ------- date for the seminar, but it
will be some time in the first week of November.
(A) full (B) factual
(C) explicit 
(D) specific
▶
组织委员会还未给研讨会定下具体的日期，但会议将在11月的第一周
举行。
▶
解析
“还没有决定研讨会的具体日期”的句意最恰当，故选 (D)
specific (具体的，特定的)。
