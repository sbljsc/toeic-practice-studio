### 第 1 题

Company strategists ------- predicted that conditions in the Middle East would
eventually stabilize and result in expanded sales.

- A. wrong
- B. wronged
- C. wrongly
- D. wrongness

## 答案 / 题解

### 第 1 题

**答案：C**

Company strategists ------- predicted that conditions in the Middle East would
eventually stabilize and result in expanded sales.
(A) wrong (B) wronged
(C) wrongly
 (D) wrongness
▶
公司策划师错误地预计中东的形势会最终稳定下来，并会带来销售规
模的扩大。
▶
解析
 横线处应填入修饰predicted的副词。答案是 (C) wrongly。
一般情况下，修饰及物动词的副词位于动词前或宾语后。
