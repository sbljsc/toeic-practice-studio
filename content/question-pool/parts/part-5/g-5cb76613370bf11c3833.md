## 选项

### 第 1 题

The lunch menu today consists of a choice of ------- chicken or pork served
with a salad or vegetables.

- A. both
- B. other
- C. either
- D. another

## 答案 / 题解

### 第 1 题

**答案：C**

The lunch menu today consists of a choice of ------- chicken or pork served
with a salad or vegetables.
(A) both (B) other
(C) either
 (D) another
▶
今天的午餐菜单上有搭配沙拉或者蔬菜的鸡肉和猪肉，可以任选其
一。
▶
解析
 横线后的chicken和pork由or连接，因此可判断是
either...or...的结构。答案是 (C)。both...and...，
neither...nor...，not only...but also...等结构也经常出现在试题中。
