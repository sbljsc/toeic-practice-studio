## 选项

##### 第 1 题

Westfield Finances believe that ------- member of each team should be
allowed to contribute his or her thoughts freely during brainstorming sessions.

- A. every
- B. other
- C. most
- D. several

## 答案 / 题解

##### 第 1 题

**答案：A**

Westfield Finances believe that ------- member of each team should be allowed
to contribute his or her thoughts freely during brainstorming sessions.
(A) every
 (B) other
(C) most (D) several
▶
怀斯特菲尔德金融公司认为，每个小组中的每位成员在集体讨论时都
应该被允许自由地表达其想法。
▶
解析
 横线处所填单词修饰单数名词member，故选 (A) every。
every后面接名词单数，常用结构为“every +名词单数+ of +名词/名词短
语”，而other，most，several后面须接名词复数。
