## 选项

##### 第 1 题

The business council stated its opinion that the new penalties for tax evasion
are out of ------- with the seriousness of the crime.

- A. size
- B. proportion
- C. equivalence
- D. combination

## 答案 / 题解

##### 第 1 题

**答案：B**

The business council stated its opinion that the new penalties for tax evasion
are out of ------- with the seriousness of the crime.
(A) size 
(B) proportion
(C) equivalence (D) combination
▶
商业委员会表达了他们的意见，认为对逃税的最新处罚措施与其罪行
的严重程度不相称。
▶
解析
 本题需选择符合句意的名词。将备选答案一一代入后分析全
句。(B) proportion(比率，均衡)和new penalties for tax evasion(对逃
税的最新处罚措施)以及the seriousness of the crime(犯罪的严重性)语
意搭配最连贯，又能和横线前后的out of和with构成固定搭配be out of
proportion with(与…不相称)，故正确答案是(B)。
