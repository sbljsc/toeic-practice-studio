## 选项

### 第 1 题

Any employee wishing to terminate his or her employment before the end of
the contract may do so after giving 30 days written -------.

- A. sign
- B. contact
- C. notice
- D. approval

## 答案 / 题解

### 第 1 题

**答案：C**

Any employee wishing to terminate his or her employment before the end of the
contract may do so after giving 30 days written -------.
(A) sign (B) contact
(C) notice
 (D) approval
▶
任何想要在合同期满之前终止雇佣关系的员工可以提前30天提交书面
通知。
▶
解析
 在合同到期前辞去工作时，需要事先提交辞职信之类的书面
通知。答案是 (C) notice (通知) 。
