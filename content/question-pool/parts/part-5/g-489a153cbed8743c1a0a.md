## 选项

### 第 1 题

Naturally, our company utilizes the production process ------- guarantees the
most satisfactory results.

- A. that
- B. who
- C. what
- D. how

## 答案 / 题解

### 第 1 题

**答案：A**

Naturally, our company utilizes the production process ------- guarantees the
most satisfactory results.
(A) that
 (B) who
(C) what (D) how
▶
我们公司自然是采用能保证得到最满意结果的生产流程。
▶
解析
 本题考查定语从句。先行词the production process (生产
流程) 在从句中作主语，横线处应填入关系代词 (A) that。
