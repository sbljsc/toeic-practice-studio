### 第 1 题

------- application procedures have resulted in a drop in interest in our
investment and other financial services packages.

- A. Complicating
- B. Complicated
- C. Complicates
- D. Complicate

## 答案 / 题解

### 第 1 题

**答案：B**

------- application procedures have resulted in a drop in interest in our
investment and other financial services packages.
(A) Complicating 
(B) Complicated
(C) Complicates (D) Complicate
▶
复杂的申请步骤已经导致人们对我们的投资和其他金融服务套餐的兴
趣下降。
▶
解析
 横线处需要填入修饰主语application procedures的形容
词。答案是 (B) complicated (复杂的) 。
