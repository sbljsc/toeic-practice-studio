## 选项

### 第 1 题

Both ------- and collectively, the employees at our company have achieved
great successes in technical as well as business innovations.

- A. substantially
- B. individually
- C. successfully
- D. expressively

## 答案 / 题解

### 第 1 题

**答案：B**

Both ------- and collectively, the employees at our company have achieved
great successes in technical as well as business innovations.
(A) substantially 
(B) individually
(C) successfully (D) expressively
▶
不论从个人还是集体方面，我们公司的员工在技术和商业创新上都取
得了很大的成功。
▶
解析
 在both A and B(A和B都)的结构中，A和B在形式、时态等方
面都必须保持一致，词义也要有对等关系。因此最恰当的是
(B)individually(个人地)。
