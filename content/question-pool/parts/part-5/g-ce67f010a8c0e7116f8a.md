## 选项

### 第 1 题

The new computerized security system never, or at least, ------- ever, requires
rebooting.

- A. hardly
- B. just
- C. overly
- D. still

## 答案 / 题解

### 第 1 题

**答案：A**

The new computerized security system never, or at least, ------- ever, requires
rebooting.
(A) hardly
 (B) just
(C) overly (D) still
▶
新的电脑化安全系统从来，或者至少几乎不需要重启。
▶
解析
 并列连词or前后有副词never和______ ever，这些副词修饰
动词requires。or连接对等的语法成分，因此横线处应填入与ever连用、词
义与never类似的副词。答案是 (A) hardly (几乎不，简直不)。hardly
ever意为“几乎从不”。
