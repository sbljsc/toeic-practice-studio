## 选项

### 第 1 题

While the Sales Department has been ------- in marketing our new range, we
have been let down by our quality assurance section.

- A. accomplishing
- B. mindful
- C. successful
- D. climbing

## 答案 / 题解

### 第 1 题

**答案：C**

While the Sales Department has been ------- in marketing our new range, we
have been let down by our quality assurance section.
(A) accomplishing (B) mindful
(C) successful
 (D) climbing
▶
虽然销售部门已经成功地将我们的新产品系列推进市场，但是我们被
质保部门拖了后腿。
▶
解析
 while既可以表示“…的时候”，也可以表示对照意义的“然
而，却”。本题中while表示“然而，却”。答案是 (C) successful，与后
面的let down形成对照意义。(A) accomplishing是及物动词，后面需要接
宾语。
