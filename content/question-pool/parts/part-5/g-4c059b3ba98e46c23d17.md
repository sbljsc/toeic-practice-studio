## 选项

##### 第 1 题

The latest ------- to emerge from our production lines is considered by many to
lead the field in the area of mobile communications.

- A. product
- B. produce
- C. producing
- D. productivity

## 答案 / 题解

##### 第 1 题

**答案：A**

The latest ------- to emerge from our production lines is considered by many to
lead the field in the area of mobile communications.
(A) product
 (B) produce
(C) producing (D) productivity
▶
从我们的生产线上生产出来的最新产品被很多人认为是引导移动通信
领域的产品。
▶
解析
 根据横线前的the latest和横线后的to emerge from our
production lines(从我们的生产线上生产出来的)可知，横线处应填入名
词。(A)、(B)、(D)都是名词，其中最符合句意的是(A)product(产品，制
品，产物)。
