## 选项

### 第 1 题

Several exciting new pieces of equipment ------- in our gymnasium, and we
invite you to come and try them out at no cost during the next week.

- A. install
- B. installed
- C. have installed
- D. have been installed

## 答案 / 题解

### 第 1 题

**答案：D**

Several exciting new pieces of equipment ------- in our gymnasium, and we
invite you to come and try them out at no cost during the next week.
(A) install (B) installed
(C) have installed 
(D) have been installed
▶
我们的健身房新增了几样新的令人兴奋的健身器材，我们邀请您下周
来免费体验。
▶
解析
 install是表示“安装；设置”的及物动词。但横线后并没有
宾语，只有地点状语in our gymnasium。主语new pieces of equipment相
当于install的宾语，因此本句应构成被动语态。答案是 (D) have been
installed。
