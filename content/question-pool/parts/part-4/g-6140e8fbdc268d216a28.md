## 原文

##### Transcript

We would like all employees to note that our management has decided to expand the snack bar into a full lunchroom service. There will be several hot main courses, salads, desserts, and hot and cold drinks on offer every day. Prices will be kept to a minimum. We trust that our employees will not only enjoy the food and atmosphere but also the convenience of having healthy and tasty meals available in the building. A daily menu will be posted on the notice board in the staff room. We hope that you will appreciate this expansion of our services to our valued employees.
