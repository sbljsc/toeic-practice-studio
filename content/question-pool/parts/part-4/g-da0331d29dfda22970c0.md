## 原文

##### Transcript

Welcome, everyone, to the thirteenth annual conference being hosted by the International Society of Electrical Engineers. My name is Kate Daniels, and I'm the current president of the society. Before we get started with the keynote speech by Dr. Lyndon Schafer, I should inform you of a couple of things. First, Sarah West will be a bit late, so she'll be changing speaking times with Meredith Trudeau. In addition, we'll be holding the election for a new president today at 5:00 in the evening. Only current members of the society are eligible to vote. You must show your membership card prior to receiving a ballot.
