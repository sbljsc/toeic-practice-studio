### Transcript

Thank you for coming to this meeting, everyone. We need to discuss the sales data from the first quarter which was released this morning. Our branches in Europe and Asia did exceptionally well. Every single one of them turned a profit in the last three months. As for our American and Canadian branches, they were mostly profitable as only a couple places lost money.
