## 原文

### Transcript

I can see that this is your first time for treatment here in our office, Mr. Anderson. Would you please take this form to the counter and fill it out as best as you can? If you have any questions, come back to me. We need this information to give you the best treatment possible. After you are done, bring it back to me, and I will place you in the queue to see Dr. Helen Thompson. She should be available in around thirty minutes.
