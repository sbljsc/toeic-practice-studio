## 原文

### Transcript

It's time for an update on the local economy. Thanks to the efforts of Mayor Crawford, several companies have opened branches in Richmond during the past year. That's been great for the economy as unemployment is at a record low. However, there's a housing shortage now, so the average price of a house has risen by more than twenty percent this year. This morning, Carter Construction announced it's building an apartment complex on the eastern side of the Mohawk River. It will be adjacent to the Danforth Bridge. Construction won't be complete until eighteen months from now, but when it's done, more than 600 family-sized units will be available for rent or purchase.
