## 原文

##### Transcript

I've got a quick update on the luncheon—we're going to be having to celebrate the successful completion of the Dumont project. I reserved a room at the restaurant at the Greenbrier Hotel, but I was informed by Ms. Anderson that CEO Richards and ten members of his staff intend to be at the luncheon. I'll be calling the hotel back to arrange a private room big enough for forty-five people. We'll also be increasing the budget since we'll have so many more people there. As soon as I make the necessary arrangements, I'll send an e-mail to each of you to provide you with the relevant data.
