##### Transcript

Hello, Jason. This is Steve Meyers. I'd like to inform you that I'm getting close to where you are right now. I just passed a sign indicating that I need to get off the interstate in six kilometers. I know the restaurant we're meeting at is close to the exit, so I estimate that I'll be there in about ten minutes. If you want to go ahead and order drinks and appetizers, please be my guest. You know what I like. I hope you brought your laptop with you. I need to show you some files, but mine is in the shop, so I wasn't able to bring it.
