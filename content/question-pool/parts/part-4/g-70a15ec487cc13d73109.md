## 原文

### Transcript

I'm pleased to report that tickets are selling rapidly, and we should sell the remaining 450 tickets by the end of the week. It appears as though everyone in town is eager to hear Jennifer Evans sing. Just so you know, the posters haven't arrived yet. Eric happened to notice a mistake as the number 23 was written on the posters instead of 13. So the printer had to make a change and threw away the posters which had already been printed. I've been informed that the posters will arrive tomorrow, so we'll send a few of you out to post them in places around town then.
