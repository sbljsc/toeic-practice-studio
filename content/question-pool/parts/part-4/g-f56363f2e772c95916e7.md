## 原文

### Transcript

Attention, everyone! The parking lot on the west side of our building will be closed starting from this Friday, January 25, until March 15, due to construction. Therefore, those employees who enter the building from Midland Avenue must use the south entrance from Jane Street. Also, we ask everyone to please park your cars in our underground garage to allow our customers access to the parking spots on the ground floor. I know this will cause some inconvenience to our salespeople, who come in and out of our building throughout the day. However, we do ask you to bear with this situation until the construction comes to its completion. Also, we'd like to make a suggestion for those employees who live near the office to leave their cars at home and take public transportation to work. One other option is to start carpooling. Anyway, think about it, and if you have any great ideas, please go and speak with our receptionist Janice anytime.

### Transcript (zh)

大家请注意！由于施工，我们大楼西侧的停车场将从1月25日（本周五）起到3月15日关闭。因此，从麦德兰大道进入大楼的员工必须从简大街上的南面入口进入。另外，所有的员工请把车停在我们的地下车库，以便我们的顾客可以使用一楼的停车场。我知道这会给我们的销售人员带来一些不便，因为他们每天经常进出大楼。但是，我们还是要请大家忍耐，等待施工结束。我们还建议住在公司附近的员工把车留在家里，乘坐公共交通工具上班。另一个选择就是拼车上班。总之，考虑一下，如果您有什么好的想法，请随时告诉我们的接待员贾尼丝。
