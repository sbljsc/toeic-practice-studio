## 原文

### Transcript

Today your community radio station is very pleased to invite you to the fifth CCR Veteran and Vintage Car Rally. Fifty-four absolutely beautiful, fully-restored vehicles from the beginning of the twentieth century will make their way from the rally point, in Federation Square, down to Exhibition Palace in Victoria Gardens. Cars will be arriving at Federation Square at 9:00 a. m. and will leave for Victoria Gardens at eleven o'clock. If you arrive at around 9:00 a. m., you will have a chance not only to inspect the cars but also to meet their owners. Entry into the Exhibition Building will cost five dollars for adults and two dollars for seniors and children under twelve years old. Don't miss this fabulous occasion!
