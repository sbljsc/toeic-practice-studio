## 原文

### Transcript

It's come to my attention that our latest advertising campaign isn't doing well. In fact, sales of several of our items declined right after our newest TV commercials started airing. According to customer feedback, the ads are extremely unappealing and make people not want to purchase our products. As such, I'm canceling the ad campaign and firing Walters Media. We need to start a new media campaign to make up for the damage that has been done at once. I've decided to use Pembroke Advertising. Janet, I want you to contact the company at once and request that a representative be sent here. Ideally, someone should arrive tomorrow morning.
