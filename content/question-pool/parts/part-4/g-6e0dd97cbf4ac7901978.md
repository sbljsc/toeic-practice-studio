## 原文

##### Transcript

It is reported in the business news column today that Susan Bartlett, CEO of Tasty Foods, will leave her position at the end of December. It is well known that the success of Tasty Foods' expansion into Asia was due to Ms. Bartlett's personal dynamism and commitment. A committee, which includes Ms. Bartlett, who will remain with the company as a consultant, is currently searching for the next CEO.
