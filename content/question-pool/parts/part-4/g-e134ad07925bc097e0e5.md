## 原文

### Transcript

Today is the first workday of the new year, and we'd like to start off with a very positive resolution, especially as we have twenty new staff members beginning today. Our company has always been heavily involved in volunteer work. We have several teams of volunteers, each of which specializes in a different area. Please join one of the clubs and participate in their special activities. We'd like all members to remember that service to others can be surprisingly fun and rewarding. Our receptionist, Jo Workman, who is also the leader of the environmental action team, has prepared a handbook on volunteering and other social activities. Please get a copy, and don't hesitate to join.
