## 原文

##### Transcript

The Brandywine Café is pleased to announce that the renovations on the building are complete, so we'll be reopening for business this Saturday. All weekend long, if you buy any type of beverage, you'll get a free snack of your choice. So come to the corner of Madison Road and Lancaster Street and check out the new and improved Brandywine Café. We've even added fruit drinks to our menu since summer is rapidly approaching. Cool off by ordering a strawberry smoothie or other similar drink. You can enjoy your drink at a table on the outdoor patio we added on the second floor. Our hours are from 8 a.m. to 10 p.m. every day.
