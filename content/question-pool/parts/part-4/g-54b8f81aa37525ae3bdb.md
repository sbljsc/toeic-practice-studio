## 原文

### Transcript

Hello. My name is Richard Armstrong. You may recall that I picked up a few books at your store this afternoon. I need to report a slight problem with one. When I opened the plastic wrapper to read the book, I noticed that several pages in it were torn. Since it cost me $25, I'd really like to have my money refunded or receive a brand-new copy. I'll be visiting the store again tomorrow with the book, but I thought that I ought to call ahead of time so that you'll be expecting me. Thanks a lot. Goodbye.
