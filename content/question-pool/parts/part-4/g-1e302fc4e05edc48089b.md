##### Transcript

Before I end this meeting, let me remind everyone that the annual summer picnic will take place this Saturday. It'll be at Griswold Park from 11 a.m. to 4:00 p.m. If you need a ride there, a bus is going to be picking people up here at 10:30 in the morning. Just wait between the employee parking lot and the western entrance to the main building. As for the picnic, we'll have the usual food, and we'll also play a wide variety of games and do other activities. Your family members are welcome to attend, but please contact Janet Hoff at extension 584 to inform her of how many people will be accompanying you.
