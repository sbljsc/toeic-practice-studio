##### Transcript

Hello. This is Ryan Rogers from the Murdoch Corporation. I'm calling to let you know that you have been selected for an interview for the position you applied for. Your interview has been scheduled for next Tuesday morning at 10:30. If this doesn't work for you, please call me back at 874-8432 no later than this Thursday so that we can reschedule it. In addition, you must bring three letters of recommendation from current or previous employers with you. You failed to include those in the application packet you submitted, and we won't be able to hire you without them.
