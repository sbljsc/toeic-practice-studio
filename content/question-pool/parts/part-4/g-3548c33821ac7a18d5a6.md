## 原文

### Transcript

In sports news today, Blackburn United announced their intention to recruit new players and expand their facilities after their historic Cup Final win on Friday night. Their win has attracted the attention of major new sponsors and investors from the corporate world. There are rumors that two star strikers now playing with European teams have signed or are close to signing. New coaching staff members will also be recruited, and training facilities and clubrooms will be upgraded, a club spokesperson announced today.
