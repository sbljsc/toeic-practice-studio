## 原文

##### Transcript

A benefit concert for the Blue Hills Animal Shelter will be held Friday July 17th at the Orion Theater. Many of your favorite artists, performers, and musicians will be there. We are also proud to announce that the event will be recorded as part of CBS Television's special concert series and will be released on CD and DVD. All proceeds will be donated to the animal shelter so that it can continue its great work. So don't be late! Buy your tickets today, and you can be part of this historic event.
