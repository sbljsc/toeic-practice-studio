##### Material 1

### Halls will Come to Life with Music

Albert Hall and Royal Hall have disclosed their schedules for the upcoming
season, with everything {{blank:1}} -------- classical music to stand-up comedy acts
and lots in between.

{{blank:2}} --------. The venerable venue {{blank:3}} -------- will host Bruce Thornton
on January 17, Ron Ghanem on January 3, Terry Lightfoot on January 9, and folk
superstar Judith Bachman on February 12, 13, and 14.

At Royal Hall, in its classical {{blank:4}} --------, American soprano Rosemary
Voigt, Canadian soprano Jonathon Pierre, Canadian baritone Mel Finley, and
Polish contralto George Podles are on the bill.

## 选项

##### 第 1 题

- A. over
- B. at
- C. up
- D. from

##### 第 2 题

- A. American Soprano Rosemary Voigt is very popular among people all over
the world.
- B. The Smiths are set to make their debut at 100-year-old Albert Hall.
- C. Albert Hall and Royal Hall both have great seating capacity.
- D. The folk superstar Judith Bachman is more famous than the Canadian
baritone Mel Finley.

##### 第 3 题

- A. besides
- B. of
- C. also
- D. either

##### 第 4 题

- A. series
- B. steps
- C. occasions
- D. separations

## 答案 / 题解

##### 第 1 题

**答案：D**

Albert Hall and Royal Hall have disclosed their schedules for the upcoming
season, with everything -------- classical music to stand-up comedy acts and lots in
between.
(A) over (B) at
(C) up 
(D) from
→classical music to stand-up comedy acts and lots in between
是演出内容，classical music和stand-up comedy acts是不同种类的艺术
形式。按照句意，应该使用表示范围的from... to... (从…到…)。答案是
(D)。

##### 第 2 题

**答案：B**

(A) American Soprano Rosemary Voigt is very popular among people all over
the world.
(B) The Smiths are set to make their debut at 100-year-old Albert Hall.
(C) Albert Hall and Royal Hall both have great seating capacity.
(D) The folk superstar Judith Bachman is more famous than the Canadian
baritone Mel Finley.
→下句说“这个庄严肃穆的音乐厅也将于……举办……”。可知，横线
处句子应该描述音乐厅的演出信息，故选 (B)。

##### 第 3 题

**答案：C**

The venerable venue -------- will host Bruce Thornton on January 17, Ron
Ghanem on January 3, Terry Lightfoot on January 9, and folk superstar Judith
Bachman on February 12, 13, and 14.
(A) besides (B) of
(C) also
 (D) either
→前一句提到史密斯一家将在阿尔伯特音乐厅首次登台，之后又介绍了
几位艺术家的演出安排，故最恰当的是 (C) also。

##### 第 4 题

**答案：A**

At Royal Hall, in its classical --------, American soprano Rosemary Voigt,
Canadian soprano Jonathon Pierre, Canadian baritone Mel Finley, and Polish
contralto George Podles are on the bill.
(A) series
 (B) steps
(C) occasions (D) separations
→横线后列举了女高音歌唱家、男中音歌唱家、女低音歌唱家的名字，
因此 (A) series (系列) 最恰当。
