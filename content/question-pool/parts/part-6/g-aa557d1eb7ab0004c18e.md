#### Diet & Diabetes Research Study

At St. Vincent’s Hospital in Kansas, we are planning to conduct a six-month
{{blank:1}} -------- comparing the potential benefits of two dietary approaches to {{blank:2}}

Twenty individuals currently taking tablets for diabetes but who have clean

## 答案 / 题解

##### 第 1 题

**答案：D**

At St. Vincent’s Hospital in Kansas, we are planning to conduct a six-month ---
----- comparing the potential benefits of two dietary approaches...
(A) work (B) insight
(C) glance 
(D) study
→选择充当conduct的宾语的名词。conduct意为“为证明特定信息或事
实而实施行动”，故选 (D) study。

##### 第 2 题

**答案：A**

...comparing the potential benefits of two dietary approaches to -------- blood
sugar.
(A) reduce
 (B) reducing
(C) be reduced (D) to reduce
→to ______ blood sugar是修饰名词短语dietary approaches的不定
式，因此横线处应填入动词原形 (A) reduce。

##### 第 3 题

**答案：A**

Twenty individuals currently taking tablets for diabetes but who have clean
bills of health are -------- in the Kansas area.
(A) sought
 (B) accepted
(C) approved (D) in demand
→必须阅读下一句If you feel that you want to participate in
this study, please get in contact with Dr. Sullivan’s research
group at St. Vincent’s Hospital 才能找出正确答案。句意应为“在堪
萨斯地区寻找20位志愿者”，故选 (A) sought。

##### 第 4 题

**答案：A**

(A) The number is 755-8989.
(B) The purpose is to reduce blood sugar.
(C) Participants must have clean bills of health.
(D) The study is about diabetes.
→由上句说“如果您愿意……请联系……”可知，横线处应填沙立文医
生研究小组的联系方式，故选 (A)。
