### Experienced Office/Administration Assistant

Energetic, new public relations agency is looking for a {{blank:1}} --------
talented Office/Administrative Assistant to join and help organize a small but
rapidly growing team. You will have at least 2-3 years office experience, be a
self-starter with a ‘can-do’ attitude, and have the ability to bring order to chaos!
Duties will include: word processing such as letters, reports, and proposals;

consultants and other general office management duties. {{blank:2}} -------- experience

essential. To be successful in this role, you will have excellent communication
skills, be {{blank:3}} -------- in all Microsoft applications, particularly PowerPoint,

## 答案 / 题解

##### 第 1 题

**答案：D**

Energetic, new public relations agency is looking for a -------- talented
Office/Administrative Assistant to join and help organize a small but rapidly
growing team.
(A) highness (B) higher
(C) highest 
(D) highly
→横线处需填入修饰形容词talented的副词。答案是 (D) highly (高
度地，非常地)。

##### 第 2 题

**答案：A**

-------- experience as an administrative assistant in the PR or media industry is
of value, it is not essential.
(A) While
 (B) If
(C) Whether (D) Since
→从句experience as an... is of value和主句it is not essential
形成对照。答案是 (A) while (而，却)。

##### 第 3 题

**答案：C**

To be successful in this role, you will have excellent communication skills, be
-------- in all Microsoft applications, particularly PowerPoint, and have an
outstanding eye for details.
(A) accomplished (B) gifted
(C) proficient
 (D) qualified
→本句叙述了有关职位的应聘资格，因此最符合句意的是 (C)
proficient (熟练，精通)。

##### 第 4 题

**答案：C**

(A) At least three years’ practical experience is required for the position.
(B) The applicant must be independent and is able to deal with emergencies.
(C) The position is open to both part-time and full-time applicants.
(D) The experience as a senior manager in the PR is not essential.
→下句交代该职位的工作特点。横线处也应是对该职位特点的相关描
述，故选 (C)。选项 (A) 和 (B) 前面已经提到了，选项 (D) 与原文内容
不符。
