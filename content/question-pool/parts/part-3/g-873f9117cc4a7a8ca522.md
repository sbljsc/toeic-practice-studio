## 原文

### Transcript

W: I overheard you mentioning to the sales manager that you and your wife are into watching movies. Will you be seeing one this weekend by any chance?
M: Yes, I was thinking of checking out the Sunday matinée at the Imperial Six Cinema. They are playing Mary Poppins there this weekend. You see, I am a real big fan of classical flicks.
W: Oh, that's perfect. I got these two complimentary tickets that you can use at that theater. My brother works there, and he gave them to me, but I have to go out of town this weekend. Why don't you put them to good use?
M: Really? That's very nice of you. I really appreciate it.
