## 原文

### Transcript

W: I was impressed with the interviewee we met this morning. He has plenty of experience and should be easy to collaborate with.
M: The only problem is that Matt Doering can't start working until two months from now.
W: Do you think we can afford to wait that long? I suppose we could hire Harry Anderson instead, but he's not as qualified.
M: I'm in favor of waiting until Matt can start. In the meantime, we can give extra work to Sylvia. She doesn't mind working overtime.
W: All right, then do you want to make the call, or should I?
M: Go ahead and do it. Let me know if our offer is accepted.
