## 原文

### Transcript

W: I have an appointment to meet with Mr. Ewing at 2. Where can I park?
M: I'm sorry, but our underground parking lot is under renovation at the moment. There is free parking two blocks north of here, but there is a good chance that it might be a full house today because of the art exhibit. Your best bet is to turn your car around and drive back down this street to the intersection. There is a pay lot there.
W: That's quite a distance to walk back here. Do you think I would get a ticket if I just parked somewhere along this street?
M: You can try, but I wouldn't if I were you. I saw a car being towed away a few days ago.
