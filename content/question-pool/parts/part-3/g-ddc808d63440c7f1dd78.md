## 原文

##### Transcript

M: Ms. Collins is considering restructuring the manner in which we acquire books for the library.
W: Why is that? Is there a problem?
M: Sort of. Take a look at this graph. The section with the least number of books is also the busiest because patrons are checking them out so much.
W: Hmm...We should definitely be spending more money in that case. How soon can we start purchasing more books?
M: We've almost spent the money allocated for acquisitions for the year, so we should wait two more months before buying anything new.
W: Well, meanwhile, let's compile a list of which books we want to acquire.
