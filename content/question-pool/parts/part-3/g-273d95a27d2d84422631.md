##### Transcript

W: That's for the room service you had this morning, sir.
M: Room service? What room service? You'd better check your computer again because I think you're mistaking me for someone else. I met a client for breakfast at a restaurant this morning.
W: Oh, is that right? Let me straighten this mistake out right away.
