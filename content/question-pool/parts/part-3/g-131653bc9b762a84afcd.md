##### Transcript

M: As you can see, Ms. Bright, the Emerald Room has space for fifty people, so that should be large enough for the seminar you're hosting.
W: Actually, we've had more than seventy people sign up so far. It appears as though we may have more than 100 attendees.
M: The Diamond Room has a capacity of 150. It's our biggest room, but it's rented out on December 11.
W: How about on the tenth?
M: It's available the entire day. Perhaps it would be better to book it on an alternative date.
W: I pretty much have to, don't I? But I need to speak with my supervisor first to get confirmation.
