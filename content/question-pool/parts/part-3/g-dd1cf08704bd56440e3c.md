##### Transcript

W: Before we board our plane, do you mind if we do some shopping at the duty-free stores? I need to buy some souvenirs for my friends.
M: Be my guest. But I've got to head to the gate to speak with somebody there. Take a look at my boarding pass.
W: Oh, I can't believe one of the letters in Thomas is missing.
M: Yeah, I want to make sure there aren't any problems when it's time to board. Where shall we meet when I'm done?

M: Great. I'll see you in a few minutes.
