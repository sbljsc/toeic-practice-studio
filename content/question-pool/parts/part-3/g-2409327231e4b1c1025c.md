## 原文

### Transcript

W: I must have eaten too much last night at the buffet restaurant because my stomach still feels bloated. I'm just going to settle for coffee this morning. What are you going to have?
M: I am starved. I am going to order the continental breakfast.
W: Wow, you really do have an appetite, don't you? I mean, I don't understand how you can eat as much as you do yet keep yourself so thin like that. You are so lucky.
M: Well, I guess I have always digested my food very fast. I've been thin for as long as I can remember. Actually, when I was younger, my wish was to gain some weight. In fact, I remember eating 5 meals a day during high school just to gain some weight, but I didn't have any luck.

W: 我昨晚在自助餐馆一定是吃多了，胃到现在还觉得胀。今天早晨我就勉强喝点咖啡吧。你打算吃点什么？
M: 我饿极了。我打算去点欧式早餐。
W: 哇，你很有胃口嘛，是吧？我是说，我不理解为什么你吃这么多，还能保持那么瘦。你真是幸运。
