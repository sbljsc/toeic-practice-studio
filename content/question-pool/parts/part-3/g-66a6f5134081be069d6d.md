## 原文

### Transcript

W: What do you think of this item? This is one of the nicest in our collection.
M: The style is really attractive, and I like the fact that it's made of gold, but it's somewhat more than I'm willing to spend.
W: What would you say if I offered it to you at twenty percent off the sticker price?
M: I can do that.
W: Wonderful. Would you like for me to wrap this necklace up for you?
M: That would be perfect. I'm going to give it to my wife at dinner tonight. We're celebrating our tenth anniversary.
