## 原文

### Transcript

M: Mr. Venters has granted everyone in our department permission to attend the trade fair this Friday.
W: That's wonderful news. I'll be out of town, so I can't make it, but I'm sure many people are eager to go.
M: That's right. I already asked around, and seventeen people indicated they'll be going. I'll be registering everyone later today.
W: I don't think we've ever had that many people attend a professional event.
M: More people are focusing on networking these days, and trade fairs are some of the best places to do that.
W: I suppose you're right. In that case, it's a real shame I can't go.
