## 原文

### Transcript

M: Hello, Ms. Loomis. This is Brandon Carter calling. The items you ordered from us on Monday have just arrived.
W: That's surprising news. They weren't supposed to arrive until sometime next week.
M: One of our employees visited the warehouse this morning, so he picked up all the items and brought everything back in his truck.
W: Wonderful. You even got the couch and armchair I ordered?
M: Of course. If you're at home today, we can deliver the shipment to you anytime between two and six.
W: I'll be home by five o'clock.
M: Great. I'll make the arrangements.

- A. At the post office.
- B. At a furniture store.
- C. At an appliance store.
- D. At a stationery store.

### 第 2 题

Why is the woman surprised?

- A. Ordered items arrived early.
- B. A price is lower than expected.
- C. A rare item is available.
- D. Delivery will be made for free.

What does the man imply when he says, “I’ll make the arrangements”?

- A. He will obtain all the items the woman wants.
- B. Some items will be installed in the woman’s home.
- C. The woman’s items will be delivered in the evening.
- D. He can have some items returned to the warehouse.
