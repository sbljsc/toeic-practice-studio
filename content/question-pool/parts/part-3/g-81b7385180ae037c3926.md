## 原文

### Transcript

M: I heard we're going to be sending some employees to the trade fair next week.
W: That's right. Mr. Wilson decided to rent a booth since so many international firms will be represented there.
M: I'd love to go. I've been to several trade fairs in the past, so I'm familiar with what happens at them.
W: You'd better drop by his department and talk to him soon in that case. All attendees must be registered by the end of the day tomorrow.
M: I wasn't aware of that. Thanks for letting me know.
W: No problem.
