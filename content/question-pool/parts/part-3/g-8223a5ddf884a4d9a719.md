## 原文

##### Transcript

M: I was at the mall and saw this beautiful laptop. I couldn't help but pick it up. Don't you think it's nice?
W: Yes, it's a very nice laptop, but why did you buy it? I thought you just picked up a new one a few weeks ago.
M: I know, but I was not happy with the keyboard, so I gave it to my brother, and he gave me the money to buy this one.
W: You guys must have a lot of money. These laptops cost an arm and a leg.
