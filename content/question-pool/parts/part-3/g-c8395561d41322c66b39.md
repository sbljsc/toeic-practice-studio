### Transcript

W: I was just informed we'll be getting a new assignment. We're scheduled to go to 83 Lambert Street.
M1: What kind of work will we be doing there?
W: We've got to take care of the lawn and plant some trees and flowers. The job starts tomorrow.
M1: Okay, but I heard it's supposed to rain then. Mr. Myers, can we delay the work next week if it rains?
M2: In that case, you'll have the day off, but you'll need to come on the weekend to handle the job. It's for a new client, and we don't want to lose him. Is that okay, Tim?
M1: I sure hope it doesn't rain. I'm planning to visit my parents.
