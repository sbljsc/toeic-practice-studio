##### Transcript

W: Let me check on that, Mr. Farmer. May I have your address, please?
M: I live at 382 Washington Avenue.
W: Hmm...According to the computer, the money was withdrawn from your account yesterday, so you were sent the bill in error.
M: I can disregard it, right?
W: That's correct. And thanks for bringing this to our attention. We'll check the computer system for other problems like this.
