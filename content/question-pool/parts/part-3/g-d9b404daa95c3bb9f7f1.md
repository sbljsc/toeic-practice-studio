## 原文

##### Transcript

M: Lucy, a client of mine is arriving in a few minutes for a consultation, but Mr. Woods just requested to see me immediately.
W: What's the name of the person coming to visit?
M: Alexander Sullivan. Would you mind talking to him for a few minutes? I should be back by a quarter after eleven.
W: Okay, but please be sure the meeting doesn't run too long. Derrick and I are going to the Watertown facility, and we're planning to leave at half past eleven.
M: I'm positive I'll be done before then.
W: In that case, I'll speak with Mr. Sullivan until you return.
