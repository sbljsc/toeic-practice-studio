## 原文

##### Transcript

M: Wilma, are you planning to e-mail me those documents anytime soon? The staff meeting is thirty minutes from now.
W: I sent them to you more than two hours ago. Didn't you get them?
M: Apparently not. I just checked my e-mail, and nothing has arrived all morning.
W: We could be having problems with the company-wide computer server. I'll give someone in the Maintenance Department a call right now.
M: Okay, but before you do that, how about printing the documents and then bringing them to me? I need to look over them at once.
W: No problem. You'll have them in two minutes.
