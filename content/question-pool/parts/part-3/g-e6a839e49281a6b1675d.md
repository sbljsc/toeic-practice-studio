##### Transcript

M: Hello, Ms. Wilson. This is Mark Daniels. I regret to inform you that I'll be a few minutes late for our meeting.
W: What's the problem? Are you stuck in traffic?
M: No, but I missed the exit for your office, so I had to drive for ten minutes until I could turn around. I should be there in fifteen minutes.
W: Okay, but please be aware that I have to leave by 2:30. I'm meeting with the CEO of my firm then. Will you have enough time for the demonstration?
M: More than enough. You'll be able to see everything our new laptop can do.
W: Great. I'm looking forward to it.

- C. He went the wrong way.
- D. He got caught in traffic.

- A. Negotiate a price.
- B. Meet an executive.
- C. Make an offer.
- D. Sign a contract.

What does the man most likely mean when he says, “More than enough”?

- A. The woman’s counteroffer is acceptable.
- B. He brought several products with him.
- C. He can finish the demonstration by 2:30.
- D. The room he will use is the right size.
