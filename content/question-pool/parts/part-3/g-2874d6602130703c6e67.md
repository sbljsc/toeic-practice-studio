##### Transcript

W: Hello. This is Cindy Wells. I've got an appointment with a dentist tomorrow at 3:30 p.m., but I'm in lots of pain. Is it possible to see someone today?
M: I'm sorry to hear that, Ms. Wells. According to the schedule, we have one open slot today. It's at 1:00 p.m. However, it won't be with your regular dentist.
W: That's fine. I don't believe I can wait until tomorrow.
M: Okay, I'll go ahead and cancel your Tuesday appointment then. Oh, could you please bring your insurance card?
W: Why do you need it? I thought you already had my records.
M: We're upgrading the computer system today, so we can't go online until tomorrow.
