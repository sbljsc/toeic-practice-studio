### Transcript

M: Excuse me, but I'd like to exchange this shirt I bought last night.
W1: Of course, sir. Do you mind my asking what the problem is?
M: Not at all. When I arrived home and took it out of the packaging, I noticed this huge tear in it.
W1: Wow, I'm terribly sorry about that. You're free to exchange it for anything of equal or lesser value here. May I see your receipt, please?
M: I didn't realize you were going to request it.
W2: You can let him exchange it, Danielle. I sold him the item yesterday, so I can confirm he bought it here.
M: Thanks for your assistance.
