- A. He attended a meeting.
- B. He delivered a speech.
- C. He visited a factory out of town.
- D. He worked on the budget.

What does the man imply when he says, “I’m scheduled to be in Laredo all
day”?

- A. He will miss a scheduled meeting.
- B. He cannot submit the budget on time.
- C. He hopes an event will be rescheduled.
- D. He wants the women to accompany him to go to the meeting.

What does Cindy offer to do for the man?

- A. Contact Mr. Richards.
- B. Provide him with information.
- C. Review the numbers in the budget.
- D. Help him write a speech.
