##### Transcript

M: I picked up a cell phone here the other day, but there is something the matter with the screen. It keeps blinking out all the time. I would like an exchange on it, please.
W: Of course, sir. Why don't you give that to me, and I can get you a new one from the back room right away?
M: Actually, I was wondering if I could take another model instead? I was told that the new X-100 model has far more functions than the one I got. Do you have it in stock today?
