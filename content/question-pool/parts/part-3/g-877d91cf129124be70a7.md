## 原文

### Transcript

M: Have we made arrangements to reschedule last week's outdoor concert which was canceled due to rain?
W: I was informed by Travis Donovan that it will be held next Tuesday at 6:00 in the evening.
M: Are you positive? I want to print some posters to advertise it and don't want to get the date wrong.
W: Why don't you call Mr. Donovan at extension 584 and confirm it with him?
M: Thanks for the number. But I have to wait to call him because I just saw him leave the office with a client.
W: Oh, I wasn't aware he had any meetings scheduled for today.
