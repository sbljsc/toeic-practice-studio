## 原文

### Transcript

M: I just received a call from Larry from our head office, and he said Mr. Wang can't give the presentation tomorrow at our company's annual conference being held at the Prince Hotel because of a sudden illness in his family. He asked me to take his place at the conference. Would you happen to know if there is a bus or subway that goes directly from here?
W: I didn't know that you were going to give a speech there. In fact, Mr. Singh and I are going to be in attendance, and we are planning to drive. If you'd like, you can ride along with us.
M: That sounds wonderful. What time will you be taking off?
W: We will meet here at 7 since it only takes about an hour to get there. We might even have time for breakfast once we get there.

### 第 1 题

Why won’t Mr. Wang be able to give the speech?

- A. He suddenly came down with an illness.
- B. He does not have a way to get to the hotel.
- C. He has to attend to a family member.
- D. He has to make an unexpected trip.

### 第 2 题

What does the man imply when he says, “He asked me to take his place at
the conference”?

- A. He will drive the woman to the conference.
- B. He will visit Mr. Wang at the hospital.
- C. He will fill in for Mr. Wang.
- D. He will make breakfast for the guests.

### 第 3 题

How will the man get to the conference?

- A. By car.
- B. By train.
- C. By bus.
- D. By airplane.
