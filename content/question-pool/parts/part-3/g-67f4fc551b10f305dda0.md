### Transcript

W: Did you see the list of activities taking place at the community center that was released today?
M: Not yet. Is there anything on there that looks interesting? I'd love to do some fun activities this summer.
W: I'm thinking of taking part in the fishing contest. Would you like to join me? We can be on the same team.
M: Okay, that sounds good to me. Do we have to pay a fee to enter?
W: I don't, because I'm a member of the community center, but since you don't belong to it, it will cost you $15 to enter.
M: Okay, I don't mind paying that amount of money.
