## 原文

### Transcript

W: Jason, what are you planning to do this evening? Some of us are going to see a movie together.
M: I've got tickets to see a baseball game tonight.
W: Oh, that sounds like a lot of fun. I hope you enjoy it. By the way, have you finished the report yet?
M: I've written everything and checked it for mistakes, but I need to insert a couple of graphs. I should be done with that in ten minutes.
W: Great. Would you e-mail me a copy when you're finished, please?
M: Of course. That's not a problem at all.
