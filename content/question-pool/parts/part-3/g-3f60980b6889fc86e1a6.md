## 原文

##### Transcript

W: Welcome back to the office, Greg. How was your trip to Milan?
M: It didn't go quite as well as I had expected. I had been hoping the client would sign a contract, but he wants to negotiate more.
W: It sounds like you'll be returning there soon.
M: Yeah, I'm scheduled to meet Mr. Roberts next Thursday. But I've got to visit Frankfurt before I go back to Milan.
W: Good luck. If there's anything I can do to help, all you have to do is ask.
M: I'll probably take you up on that offer. I've got two other international customers who need to be visited, but I don't have time.
