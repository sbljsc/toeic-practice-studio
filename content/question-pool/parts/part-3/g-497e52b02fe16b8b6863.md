## 原文

##### Transcript

W: Pardon me, but are you giving any more tours of the gallery this afternoon?
M: Yes, there's one more, but it won't start until twenty minutes from now. Would you like to go on it?
W: Definitely. I guess I can browse around the gift shop while I wait for it to start. Where do I need to be when the tour begins?
M: Come right back here. Would you please sign your name on the sheet so that you can reserve a spot on the tour?
W: I need to do that? How come?
M: This tour is limited to twenty people since it's being given by the curator. He doesn't often do that.
