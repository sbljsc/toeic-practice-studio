## 原文

### Transcript

W: The sale is going to start tomorrow. Has everything that's being discounted been put out on display?
M: Not yet. We still need to set up a few racks in the men's clothing section. I can take care of that after my break.
W: Do you think you can handle it by yourself?
M: You have my word.
W: That's good to hear. I'm going to tell Rick and Stephanie to set up the signs so that all of our shoppers know about the sale.
M: Excellent. We should also leave flyers by the cash registers for customers to take.
