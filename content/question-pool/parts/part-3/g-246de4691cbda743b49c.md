## 原文

### Transcript

W: How is your newest employee working out?
M: Do you mean Ted? He's doing really well. Thanks so much for recommending him to me.
W: We both worked at Lensman Technology a couple of years ago, so I'm familiar with the quality of the work he does.
M: He definitely works harder than anyone else in my department. In fact, he'll probably get a promotion pretty soon.
W: Already? That's rather fast, isn't it?
M: Somewhat, but he should be rewarded for doing such good work. And it might inspire the others in my department to try a bit harder as well.
