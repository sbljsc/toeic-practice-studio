## 原文

### Transcript

W: Craig, I'm driving to Carter Pharmaceuticals to give a product demonstration today. I know you've been there before. Do you happen to know where I can park?
M: You'd be better off taking the bus. Parking in that area is hard to find.
W: I know, but I'll be bringing a couple of boxes with me, so there's no way I can take them on the bus.
M: In that case, you'd better park in the pay lot on Wilson Avenue. It's across from the Murray Building.
W: That's sort of far from Carter Pharmaceuticals, isn't it?
M: It's two blocks away, but it's the closest lot to where you're heading.
