## 原文

### Transcript

M: Hello, Ms. Maxwell. You wanted me to visit to talk about something?
W: That's correct. I need to talk about the tenants upstairs in the unit above me. They keep making too much noise late at night.
M: I see. Have you spoken with them in person to request that they be quieter?
W: I have, but that didn't work. They still listen to music and play the guitar at all hours of the day and night.
M: Okay, I'd better pay a visit to 4C and have a talk with them. When I'm done, I'll come back down here.
W: Thanks. I really appreciate your assistance.
