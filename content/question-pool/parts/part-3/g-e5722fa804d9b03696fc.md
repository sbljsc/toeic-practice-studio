## 原文

##### Transcript

M: Hi, Suzie. I'm just ringing to see if you got the job.
W: Oh, hi John. Well, actually, I'm not sure. But I do have to go for a second interview this afternoon.
M: That's great! I guess you must be excited. Where is your second meeting supposed to happen anyway?
W: At the big building next to the art gallery downtown. Hey, do you think we can meet up for lunch today before I go to my interview? I really could use some inside information about your supervisor. He is going to interview me today.
