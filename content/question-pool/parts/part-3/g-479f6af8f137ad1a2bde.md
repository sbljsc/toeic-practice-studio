## 原文

##### Transcript

W: Welcome to the Hatfield House. Have you had the opportunity to look over the menu?
M: I have, but I'm not sure what to order. It's been several months since I dined here. What do you recommend?
W: Why don't you try our newest item? You can get a pork chop with a baked potato and sautéed vegetables. I've had it before and highly recommend it.
M: All right, I think I'll go with your suggestion.
W: Excellent. In the meantime, would you care for a soup or salad as an appetizer?
M: No thanks, but I would like some iced tea.
W: Sure, I'll bring you a glass in a couple of minutes.
