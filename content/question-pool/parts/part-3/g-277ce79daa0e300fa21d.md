##### Transcript

M: Samantha, you're running a bit late this morning.
W: I had trouble driving here. I normally do a U-turn at the intersection of Maple Street and Forest Avenue, but there was a sign indicating it's not permitted anymore.
M: Oh, yeah, the city put it up because several accidents have happened at that intersection in the past few months.
W: I wasn't aware of that, so I had to drive straight until I could find a place to turn around, and that was when traffic got bad.
M: Well, it's okay to be late today since you didn't know about the sign, but make sure that it doesn't happen again.
W: Thanks for understanding.
