## 原文

##### Transcript

M: I couldn't attend the meeting this morning. Do you know when we are supposed to show up at the restaurant this evening?
W: Well, Jack told everyone to be there by 7:00. He also said for any of us to call him if we are going to be late.
M: Is that right? I have been asked to drop off some packages at our head office by Mr. Johnson, and I am not sure if I will be able to make it there on time.
W: You know, if you are driving out there now, your best bet is to call Jack. Highway 10 usually gets pretty jammed during the time you come back.
