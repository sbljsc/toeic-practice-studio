## 原文

### Transcript

W: Jonathan, if you have not reserved your flight tickets yet, you'd better do it soon. I was just told by Sarah that it was going to be difficult to get seats from next week.
M: Oh? Why is that?
W: Well, the International Film Festival is being held in New York during the same week as our conference. This event apparently attracts thousands of people from all walks of life into the city, and all the flights and hotel rooms get booked.
M: Is that right? In that case, I'd better call right now and book a seat. I don't want to miss the conference.
