W: Television sales have increased dramatically recently.
M: All the credit for that goes to our newest TV, the HD5000. TV sales reached their lowest point during the month it came out, but they've been going up ever since.
W: I had no idea. Are we running any special ads?
M: Not really. We're advertising on TV, radio, and the Internet, but we aren't spending more money than usual. Mostly, people are purchasing the HD5000 because it's so well made.
W: That's excellent news. Could you copy that sales report for me?
M: No problem, but I'll need to run upstairs because our copier is out of toner.
