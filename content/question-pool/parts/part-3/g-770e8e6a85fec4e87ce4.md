## 原文

##### Transcript

W: I've got some outstanding news. The inspector gave my new café a passing grade, so now I have permission to open it for business.
M: I'm so happy for you. When is the grand opening scheduled for?
W: This Friday. Why don't you stop by if you have some time? Drinks will be half off, and there will be live entertainment all day long.
M: Okay. I'll visit sometime after work. Where exactly is it located?
W: It's on the corner of Macon Street and Bedford Drive. It's right beside the big drugstore.
M: Oh, I know exactly where it is.
