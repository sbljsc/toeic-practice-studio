W: Well, there are several reasons, but one of the main reasons is that we have lost some of our main players to our competitors. ## 选项

##### 第 1 题

What does the man imply when he says, “our numbers have taken quite a
dip compared to this time last year”?

- A. The company’s sales have slightly increased.
- B. The company’s sales took a big dive.
- C. The company’s sales remain unchanged.
- D. The company’s sales have broken all the sales record.

##### 第 2 题

What does the woman think is the main reason for the problem mentioned?

- A. The loss of their best staff.
- B. The increased price of the products.
- C. The stress of working long hours.
- D. The lack of talented people.

##### 第 3 题

According to the woman, how can the problem be solved?

- A. By offering more pay.
- B. By providing a better working environment.
- C. By reducing the employee’s work hours.
- D. By offering incentives.
