## 原文

### Transcript

M: I want to start investing some money toward my retirement years, but I am not sure what would be best. Didn't you tell me that you know of someone who was working in the field?
W: Yes, my brother is an investment manager. He helps people make the right investment choices. Do you want me to call him and set up an appointment for you?
M: Yes. Can you? I would really appreciate it if you could do it right away. In fact, I am going to be in my office all day today, so do you think you could have him call me directly here?
W: Sorry, Jack, but he went out of town on business and won't be coming back until late this evening. I will give him a shout the first thing tomorrow morning.
