Customers at Anderson Bank can open various accounts:
Silver Savings Account: Must maintain a minimum balance of $100. Pays

Platinum Savings Account: Must maintain a minimum balance of $5,000.

Silver Checking Account: Requires a minimum balance of $200. Provides

Gold Checking Account: Requires a minimum balance of $1,000. Provides

Combined Savings-Checking Account: Requires a minimum balance of

**答案：A**

(B) It does not have a minimum deposit.
(C) It provides users with free online banking.
(D) It requires the payment of money annually.

储蓄

储蓄
