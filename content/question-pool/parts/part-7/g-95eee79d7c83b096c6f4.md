##### Passage 1

Larry Welsh [9:25 A.M.]
Jake, I’m having some issues with the copier. It won’t print anything in color.

Jake Patterson [9:27 A.M.]

You haven’t heard? The copier broke down last night while Stephanie was
using it.

Larry Welsh [9:28 A.M.]

When’s it going to be repaired by the vendor? I need to make copies for my
presentation this afternoon.

Jake Patterson [9:29 A.M.]

You’d better go to the store down the street in that case. Just charge
everything to the company account.

Larry Welsh [9:30 A.M.]
All right. Thanks.

## 选项

##### 第 1 题

Why did Mr. Welsh write to Mr. Patterson?

- A. To ask him to contact a vendor
- B. To report a malfunctioning machine
- C. To inquire about a presentation
- D. To find out the company’s account number

##### 第 2 题

At 9:29 A.M., what does Mr. Patterson imply when he writes,“You’d better go
to the store down the street”?

- A. He will not return to the office this afternoon.
- B. Items must be purchased by Mr. Welsh.
- C. The copier will not be fixed soon.
- D. The store opened a few minutes ago.

## 答案 / 题解

##### 第 1 题

**答案：B**

Why did Mr. Welsh write to Mr. Patterson?
(A) To ask him to contact a vendor.
(B) To report a malfunctioning machine.
(C) To inquire about a presentation.
(D) To find out the company’s account number.
韦尔什先生为什么发消息给帕特森先生？
(A) 
为了让他联系一名供应商。
(B) 
为了汇报一台机器有故障。
(C) 
为了询问关于展示会的事情。
(D) 
为了查明公司的账号。
→韦尔什先生向帕特森先生提到复印机出了问题，并且询问供应商什么
时候能修好，因此他是要告知帕特森先生复印机出了故障。答案是(B)。
