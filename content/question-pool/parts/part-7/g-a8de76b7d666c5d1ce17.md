## 答案 / 题解

### 第 1 题

**答案：A**

What sort of document is this?
(A) A credit card statement
(B) A bank transaction record
(C) An accounting statement
(D) A withdrawal form

(A) 
信用卡对账单

(C) 
会计报表
(D) 
取款单
→这是Visa金卡的账单明细。答案是 (A)。
