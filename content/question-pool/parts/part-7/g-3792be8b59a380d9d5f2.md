## 答案 / 题解

##### 第 1 题

**答案：A**

At 11:53 A.M., what does Mr. Smith mean when he writes,“I’ve got a lunch
meeting with a client”?
(A) He cannot meet Ms. Jenkins now.
(B) He just left the office.
(C) He will be absent from work all day.
(D) He wants Ms. Jenkins to go with him.
