##### Passage 1

Alice Sims [1:04 P.M.]

Good afternoon, Chris. I arrived at the train station but don’t know where
your company is located. Which bus should I take?

Chris Ernst [1:05 P.M.]
Hello, Alice. Actually, I recommend taking a taxi here.

Alice Sims [1:06 P.M.]

There are more than thirty people waiting in line, and there are no taxis in
the queue.

Chris Ernst [1:08 P.M.]

In that case, get on bus number 67 heading west. It’ll take you an hour to get
here though.

Alice Sims [1:09 P.M.]
In that case, I’ll get off after a couple of stops and find a taxi.

Chris Ernst [1:10 P.M.]
That should work. You’ll get here much faster then.

## 选项

##### 第 1 题

Where most likely is Ms. Sims?

- A. On a train
- B. On a bus
- C. Near a train station
- D. At a subway station

##### 第 2 题

At 1:05 P.M., why does Mr. Ernst write,“I recommend taking a taxi here”?

- A. To point out the cheapest way to travel
- B. To indicate his office is not near public transportation
- C. To encourage Ms. Sims not to be late for a meeting
- D. To suggest that Ms. Sims should not take the bus

## 答案 / 题解

##### 第 1 题

**答案：C**

Where most likely is Ms. Sims?
(A) On a train
(B) On a bus
(C) Near a train station
(D) At a subway station

在公交车上

→西姆斯女士告知欧内斯特先生自己到了火车站，并询问他该坐哪路公
