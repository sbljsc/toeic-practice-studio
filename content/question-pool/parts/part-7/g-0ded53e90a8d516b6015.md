#### HOMESTEAD LANDHOLIDINGS LIMITED

We currently have opportunities available for assistant superintendent
couples. We offer a competitive salary as well as a two-bedroom apartment plus
benefits. If you are experienced, motivated, and hardworking and wish to advance
in your career, please fax your resume to 216-755-5959.

## 选项

### 第 1 题

What is NOT being offered with the job?

- A. Good wages
- B. Housing
- C. Health insurance
- D. A car

### 第 2 题

What will be the duties of the person being hired?

- A. Buying and leasing properties
- B. Providing career counseling to other workers
- C. Cleaning and maintaining an apartment complex
- D. Motivating the sales staff of the company

## 答案 / 题解

### 第 1 题

**答案：D**

What is NOT being offered with the job?
(A) Good wages
(B) Housing
(C) Health insurance
(D) A car
这份工作不提供什么？
(A) 
高工资
(B) 
住房
(C) 
健康保险

车
→公司提供不错的薪水 (A) 、拥有两间卧室的公寓 (B) 和保险 (C)。
答案是 (D)。

### 第 2 题

**答案：C**

What will be the duties of the person being hired?
(A) Buying and leasing properties
(B) Providing career counseling to other workers
(C) Cleaning and maintaining an apartment complex
(D) Motivating the sales staff of the company
